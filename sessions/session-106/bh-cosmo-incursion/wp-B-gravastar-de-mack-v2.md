## 1. My classification scheme (defined from the physics, not pre-supplied)

I classify every corpus↔framework contact into one of five categories. These are the verdict categories the physics demanded; I invented them after reading, not before.

| Tag | Meaning | Epistemic weight |
|:----|:--------|:-----------------|
| **STRUCTURAL-SHARED** | Same mathematical object plays the same physical role in both, derivable both ways | A real correspondence (rare) |
| **GENUS-RHYME** | Same algebraic genus / same words, *different* physical content, different derivation | NOT a correspondence — a coincidence of vocabulary |
| **CATEGORY-MISMATCH** | The two objects are not even the same KIND (static endpoint vs cosmogenesis transit) | A correspondence is ill-posed; report as such |
| **NO-ANALOG** | Feature present in one program, structurally absent in the other | Asymmetry to own honestly (in either direction) |
| **CORPUS-EXCEEDS** | The corpus program is more developed / more predictive / more rigorous than the framework on the shared physics | A framework gap |

The single most important methodological finding of this re-run: **almost every contact that the index files (and, by inference, the over-steered v1) would have filed as a correspondence is in fact GENUS-RHYME, CATEGORY-MISMATCH, or NO-ANALOG.** The confirmation-bias reading ("the gravastar is the GR-side image of the substrate vacuum") survives only as a vocabulary overlap.
