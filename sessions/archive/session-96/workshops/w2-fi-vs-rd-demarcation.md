### L4: Cross-Cutting — toward a demarcation predicate

**The demarcation predicate (proposed).** An RD piece falsifies an FI-survivor claim **iff it is COLLINEAR with the verdict axis** — i.e., iff the RD variation moves the observable *along* the direction in which the verdict's band boundary is defined. It is HARMLESS iff it is **TRANSVERSE (codimension ≥ 1)** to the verdict axis — iff the RD variation moves the observable in a direction that cannot reach the band boundary. Operationally, two orthogonal sub-tests:

> **DP-1 (geometry: overall-scale vs band-position).** Decompose the observable as `O^R = S(R) · I(O)` where `I(O)` is the verdict-defining invariant (a sign, a zero-count, a band-position ratio) and `S(R)` is the regulator-dependent factor. The RD piece is HARMLESS iff `S(R)` acts on a coordinate ORTHOGONAL to `I(O)`:
> - `S(R)` = pure positive multiplicative scale AND `I(O)` = sign/zero-structure ⇒ **HARMLESS** (positive-scaling stabilizes sign; WRONSKIAN-FI `K_R`, 9–11 OOM, harmless).
> - `S(R)` = sub-dominant additive term AND `I(O)` = sign of a dominant-plus-subdominant sum, with `|S(R)| < |dominant|` throughout ⇒ **HARMLESS** (SADDLE-REGINV loop −146 ≪ +59,252 tree; sign-flip but sub-dominant).
> - `S(R)` modulates the SAME ratio the band boundary tests (`I(O)` = band-position) ⇒ **VERDICT-FALSIFYING** (EFT-CONTROL f-ratio crosses r=1.0; CC-GAP PV-shift crosses eps_FI=5%).

> **DP-2 (mechanism: structural-cancellation vs empirical-FI).** Run the MULTIPLICATIVE-NORMALIZATION-CANCELLATION pre-flight (math-scripts.md K=3 MANDATORY).
> - `MNCI = True` (moments factor as `w(L)·g_n`; w cancels identically) ⇒ the within-family FI is **structural-cancellation-FI** = Phi-trivial tautology. Weak evidence — ANY w cancels.
