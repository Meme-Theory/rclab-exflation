# Consolidated Research Novelty Findings

## Part A: Five Framework Claims (2026-03-20)

### Claim 1: U(1)_7 Ungaugability via Kosmann Derivative
- **Status**: PARTIALLY KNOWN (inner/outer distinction exists), **NOVEL application**
- **Finding**: K_7 as Kosmann derivative (outer, not inner automorphism) prevents gauging at ALL orders
- **Novelty**: Novel application to U(1)_7; three independent proofs not in literature
- **Journal**: JGP / CMP (pure math)
- **Confidence**: HIGH

### Claim 2: n_s > 1 Structural from KK Tower
- **Status**: COMPONENTS KNOWN (heat kernel, KK mass spectrum), **NOVEL integration**
- **Finding**: Bare Dirac heat kernel on compact manifold gives n_s >= 1. Red tilt requires 4D dynamics
- **Novelty**: Integration + structural claim. Bare heat kernel alone not found in literature
- **Journal**: JGP / JNCG
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM

### Claim 3: Spectral Bianchi Identity
- **Status**: PARTIALLY KNOWN (Ward-Takahashi, Bianchi in GR), **UNCLEAR formulation**
- **Finding**: Gauge invariance of spectral action under SU(3)_L constrains sector-weighted spectral derivatives
- **Novelty**: Specific constraint formulation not in sources
- **Journal**: CMP / LMP
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM

### Claim 4: alpha_s = n_s^2 - 1 Structural Theorem
- **Status**: **NOT FOUND** in any literature
- **Finding**: Five independent proofs within K^2 propagators on compact Josephson lattices
- **Novelty**: Fully novel (if derivation is sound)
- **Journal**: PRD / PLB (highest priority if valid)
- **Confidence**: LOW (requires peer validation)

### Claim 5: Cl(8) Three-Way Bridge
- **Status**: PARTIALLY KNOWN (Furey program), **NOVEL unification**
- **Finding**: Berry phase gamma/pi ~ 1, order-one violation hierarchy 2^{1+k/2}, 6/7 NCG axioms all trace to Spin(8) on C^16
- **Novelty**: Three-way unification; formula 2^{1+k/2} not found in Furey's work
- **Journal**: CMP / PRD
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH
