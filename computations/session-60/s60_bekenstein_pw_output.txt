--- Section 6: Physical Interpretation ---
ANALYSIS: Why Bekenstein does NOT truncate

The Bekenstein bound S_Bek = 2*pi*R*E grows with energy E.
|E_BCS| grows as N_modes^alpha with alpha ~ 2.49 (power-law from S59).
Meanwhile, the maximum possible entropy S_max = N_modes * ln(2) grows linearly.

Since alpha > 1, the Bekenstein bound grows FASTER than the entropy.
Higher PW sectors are FURTHER from saturation, not closer.
The bound provides NO physical truncation of the PW sum.

--- Physical Explanation ---
The Bekenstein bound S_Bek = 2*pi*R*E is an upper bound on entropy.
For BCS states confined to the KK radius R_KK = 1/M_KK:
  S_Bek(L) = 2*pi*|E_BCS(L)|  (in M_KK natural units)

|E_BCS| grows as N_modes^{2.49} (superlinear).
Maximum entropy S_max = N_modes * ln(2) grows linearly.

At level 0: S_max/S_Bek = 6.4429 = 644.3%
At level 5: S_max/S_Bek = 1.740356e-04

The ratio DECREASES with level — higher sectors are exponentially
further from saturation. This is because BCS pairing grows the
binding energy faster than the Hilbert space dimension grows the
available entropy.

ONLY the (0,0) sector is CLOSE to saturation (S_max/S_Bek ~ 6.4),
but even it does not saturate (ratio < 1 would be needed).

CLASSIFICATION: NON-PHONONIC (Bekenstein bound is geometric).
The phononic spectrum enters through E_BCS, but the bound itself
is a property of the confining geometry (R_KK).
