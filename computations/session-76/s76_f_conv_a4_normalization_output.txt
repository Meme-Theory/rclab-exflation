--- Section 5: Physical interpretation — selecting the correct p ---

  The question: what power p appears in f_conv^{(4,p)}?
  This depends on WHAT OBSERVABLE the a_4 channel couples to.

    The gauge field energy density delta_rho_gauge ~ F^2 * delta(1/g^2)

    This is an ISOCURVATURE perturbation (not adiabatic).
    It does NOT couple through gravity. No M_Pl suppression.
