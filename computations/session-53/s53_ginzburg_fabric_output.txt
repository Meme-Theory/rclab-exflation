--- Section 7: Correct Physical Description ---

  Given: N_pair = 1, N_cells = 32, L/xi = 3.0457, Gi = 0.5064

  The system is a SINGLE COOPER PAIR delocalized across a 32-site lattice
  with Josephson coupling J_C2 = 0.933 M_KK.

  The correct description is NOT Ginzburg-Landau (continuum, mean-field,
  macroscopic order parameter), but rather:

  (a) TIGHT-BINDING for the pair center-of-mass motion on the 32-cell lattice.
      Bandwidth ~ 2*z*J = 2*16*0.933 = 29.856 M_KK
      This pair is NOT condensed — it is a single quantum particle on a lattice.

  (b) The S52 'Goldstone mode' (c = 0.915) is actually the pair dispersion
      omega(K) = 2*J*(1 - cos(K*a)). For small K this looks like c*K,
      but it is the kinetic energy of ONE pair, not a collective Goldstone boson
      of a macroscopic condensate.

  (c) True spontaneous U(1)_7 breaking requires the thermodynamic limit N->inf.
      For N_pair=1, the ground state has DEFINITE particle number, not
      definite phase. The phase is COMPLETELY UNCERTAIN: delta_phi = 2*pi.

  (d) The Leggett modes (inter-sector phase oscillations) require at least
      O(1) pairs PER SECTOR. With 1 pair total in 3 sectors, the pair
      occupies one sector at a time. Leggett modes are NOT SUPPORTED.
