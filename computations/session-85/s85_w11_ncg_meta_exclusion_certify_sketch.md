## I. Meta-Theorem statement (frozen vdd §II.5)

In a Connes-Chamseddine almost-commutative spectral triple (A = C^∞(M) ⊗ A_F, H, D) with compact fiber, a cohomology class `c` vanishes in a pre-registered target group `T` whenever either:

- **(Parity)** `c` sits in a Z/2-grading component orthogonal to `T`'s image-grading under the relevant characteristic-class map (Chern character, Hopf-cyclic lift, or Gysin push-forward), OR
- **(Rank)** `c` requires generation by projections of rank ≥ k in a sub-C*-algebra of A_F whose Gelfand / representation-theoretic structure forbids rank ≥ k projections.

Both exclusion types are K-theoretically structural and preserved by Paper 01 factorization under compact-fiber / product-metric hypothesis.

## IV. Independence of Lemmas

Lemma_P lives in **cyclic cohomology** (HC^* + S/B/I operators). Lemma_R lives in **topological K-theory** (K^0(X) + Swan's theorem + Gelfand duality). The two lemmas:

- Do NOT share a source module (HC^* vs K^0_top)
- Do NOT share an intermediate object (HP^* vs H^*(X, Z))
- Do NOT share an ad-hoc hypothesis (both inherit only Meta-Theorem hypotheses)

Therefore the two corollaries are **independent with the Meta-Theorem as common parent**. Neither proof step in Corollary 1 invokes Lemma_R, and vice versa. The Meta-Theorem is thus a genuine unifying statement — not a post-hoc grouping of two unrelated results.
