## Topic Map

### A. Sonic Horizons and Analog Gravity (Papers 01, 02, 07, 18, 19, 24, 27)
The program initiated by Unruh (01) showing sonic horizons are mathematically isomorphic to gravitational horizons, reviewed comprehensively by BLV (02), and experimentally confirmed by Steinhauer (07). Parker-type creation demonstrated in BEC (18) and photon fluids (19). SAW proposal (24) and Parker historical review (27) complete the chain.

### B. Emergent Spacetime from Condensates (Papers 03, 04, 05, 06)
Volovik's program (05, 03, 04) demonstrating that superfluid 3He-A supports Weyl fermions, emergent gauge fields, and an effective metric -- the direct intellectual ancestor of phonon-exflation. Jacobson (06) derives Einstein's equation from thermodynamics, connecting to spectral action = free energy.

### C. Phonon Quantum Geometry and Goldstone (Papers 12, 13, 28)
Pellitteri (12) proves quantum geometry is essential for Goldstone theorem in crystals. Sela-Cheng-Sun (13) shows Goldstone fails for vector fields on hyperbolic lattices -- structural constraint on SU(3) phonons. Maciejko (28) provides the mathematical framework via hyperbolic band theory.

### D. Richardson-Gaudin Integrability and BCS Dynamics (Papers 08, 15)
Claeys (08) is the theoretical foundation for the framework's BCS state and GGE permanence -- Richardson-Gaudin models with broken integrability. Combescot (15) provides the crossover sound velocity and Bogoliubov spectrum connecting BCS to phononic collective modes.

### E. Quantum Acoustics Experiments (Papers 09, 10, 11, 17, 22)
Ground-state phonon control (10), quantum acoustics with superconducting qubits (11), latest BAW ground-state cooling (17), cavity optomechanics review (09), and the phononic crystals roadmap (22). These establish that individual phonons are controllable quantum objects.

### F. Topological Phonons (Papers 20, 21, 28)
Phonon topology and chirality classification (20), topological metamaterials review (21), and hyperbolic band theory (28). These establish that phonon band structures carry topological invariants (Chern numbers, Berry phase) and support robust edge states -- relevant to bulk-boundary correspondence in the framework.

### G. Pilot-Wave and Hydrodynamic Analogs (Paper 14)
Bush-Oza walking droplet system. Demonstrates quantum-like phenomena (diffraction, quantization, tunneling) from classical pilot-wave mechanics. Temporal nonlocality generates apparent spatial nonlocality -- realist interpretation support.

### H. Quantum Vacuum and Casimir Phonons (Papers 16, 26)
Phonon-assisted Casimir force control via piezoelectric resonances (16). Phonon heat transfer across vacuum through quantum fluctuations (26). Both demonstrate that phonons couple directly to quantum vacuum effects.

### I. Advanced Analog Platforms (Papers 18, 19, 23, 24, 25)
BEC curved spacetime simulator (18), photon fluid creation (19), trapped ion simulation (23), SAW particle creation (24), magnon BEC (25). Multiple independent platforms confirming universality of particle creation and phonon-mediated quantum dynamics.

## Cross-Paper Equation Concordance

### Acoustic Metric
The effective metric for sound waves appears across multiple papers with consistent structure:
- **01** (Unruh): g_00 = rho*(v^2 - c_s^2), g_0i = rho*v_i, g_ij = rho*delta_ij
- **02** (BLV): Same matrix form, general derivation from wave equation Box_g phi = 0
- **04** (Volovik 2023): Akama-Diakonov form: g_mu_nu = diag(-hbar_ac^2, ell_ac^2, ell_ac^2, ell_ac^2)
- **07** (Steinhauer): ds^2 = -(v^2-c_s^2)dt^2 + 2v dt dx + dx^2 (1D BEC form)

### Hawking Temperature
The universal formula T_H = hbar*kappa/(2*pi*k_B) appears in:
- **01** (Unruh): kappa = |dv/dx| at sonic horizon
- **02** (BLV): General derivation from surface gravity
- **07** (Steinhauer): Measured T = 0.35 +/- 0.01 (dimensionless), agreement to 5%
- **27** (Ferreiro): Parker's original Bogoliubov approach yields same result

### Bogoliubov Transformation
Particle creation via vacuum mixing:
- **02** (BLV): |beta_k|^2 = particle number, universal
- **18** (Viermann): Experimental confirmation in BEC expansion
- **27** (Ferreiro): Historical origin in Parker 1966 thesis

### Quantum Geometry
Berry curvature and quantum metric in phonon spectra:
- **12** (Pellitteri): QGT = g_ij + (i/2)*Omega_ij, essential for Goldstone
- **20** (Zhang): Topological classification of phonon bands
- **28** (Maciejko): Hyperbolic generalization of Bloch momentum

### BCS / Richardson-Gaudin
- **08** (Claeys): H_RG = sum epsilon_i*n_i + g*sum S_i^+*S_j^-. Bethe ansatz exact
- **15** (Combescot): BdG spectrum in BEC-BCS crossover: E_k = sqrt((epsilon_k-mu)^2 + Delta^2)

### J. Signal Processing / Spectral Action as Filtered PSD (LT-6 Reference, S61)

Papers in `LT6-signal-processing-reference/` — the spectral action IS a filter bank applied to the Dirac eigenvalue PSD. These papers formalize the connection.

- **LT6-01** (Sakellariadou 2015): Spectral action Tr(f(D²/Λ²)) as filtered PSD. f_4 = ∫u·f(u)du controls CC. f_0 = f(0) controls gauge couplings. f_2 = ∫f(u)du controls gravity. Three moments of one transfer function.
- **LT6-02** (Livan, Novaes, Vivo 2017): RMT eigenvalue density. Wigner semicircle. rho(0) as DC component. Note: D_K has spectral gap so rho(0)=0 — the RMT universality does NOT apply to our spectrum (gapped, not semicircle).
- **LT6-03** (Akemann, Fyodorov, Savin 2025): Non-Hermitian spectral density. DC component rho(0,0) universal. Note: D_K is self-adjoint — this paper maps only if BdG (particle-hole) extension considered.

**S61 key result (LT-6 evaluation)**: Hausdorff moment constraint f_4 ≥ f_2²/(2f_0) = 0.413 from Cauchy-Schwarz. Filter shape freedom reduces CC gap by only 0.4 orders. CC is NOT in the filter — it's in the physics. But filter design theory constrains f(u) beyond f_2 = 2.34.

**Use for**: Any computation involving the cutoff function f(u), Debye cutoff, or regularization scheme. The spectral action expansion coefficients {f_0, f_2, f_4} are filter moments subject to Hausdorff positivity constraints.
