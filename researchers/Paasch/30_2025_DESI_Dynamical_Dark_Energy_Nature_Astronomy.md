## Abstract

Understanding whether cosmic acceleration arises from a cosmological constant or a dynamical component is a central goal of cosmology, and the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) enables stringent tests with high-precision distance measurements. We analyze baryon acoustic oscillation (BAO) measurements from DESI Data Release 1 (DR1) and Data Release 2 (DR2), combined with Type Ia supernovae and a cosmic microwave background (CMB) distance prior. With the larger statistical power and wider redshift coverage of DR2, the preference for dynamical dark energy does not diminish relative to DR1. Using both a shape-function reconstruction and non-parametric approaches with a Hubble parameter prior, the data consistently favor a dark energy equation of state w(z) that crosses w = -1, transitioning from w < -1 at low redshift to w > -1 at higher redshift.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### 1. DESI BAO Measurements

DESI DR2 provides BAO distance measurements from multiple tracers (BGS, LRG, ELG, QSO, Lyman-alpha) spanning redshifts 0.1 < z < 4.2. DR2 doubles the spectroscopic sample relative to DR1, with improved systematic control and wider redshift coverage.

### 2. w0-wa Parameterization

The standard CPL parameterization w(a) = w_0 + w_a(1-a) is used as a baseline. Combined with CMB and SN data, the results show preference for dynamical dark energy (w_0 > -1, w_a < 0), with the cosmological constant (w_0 = -1, w_a = 0) lying outside the 2-3 sigma contours depending on the SN dataset used.

### 3. Shape-Function Reconstruction

A model-independent reconstruction of w(z) using shape functions (correlated prior method) reveals:
- w(z) crosses -1 near z ~ 0.4-0.5
- At low redshift (z < 0.4): w < -1 (phantom-like)
- At higher redshift (z > 0.5): w > -1 (quintessence-like)
- The crossing behavior is robust across different SN datasets

### 4. Non-Parametric Approaches

Gaussian process and binned w(z) reconstructions confirm the crossing behavior, showing it is not an artifact of the CPL parameterization.

### 5. Consistency Between DR1 and DR2

The preference for dynamical dark energy persists from DR1 to DR2 with comparable or increased significance, indicating the signal is not a statistical fluctuation that washes out with more data.

## Key Results

1. DESI DR2 BAO + CMB + SN data consistently prefer dynamical dark energy over a cosmological constant at 2-3 sigma significance.
2. The dark energy equation of state w(z) exhibits a crossing of w = -1 near z ~ 0.4-0.5.
3. The preference does not diminish from DR1 to DR2, suggesting a genuine cosmological signal.
4. Shape-function and non-parametric reconstructions confirm the w = -1 crossing is not an artifact of the CPL parameterization.
5. The results are robust across different SN compilations (Pantheon+, DES-SN5YR, Union3).
