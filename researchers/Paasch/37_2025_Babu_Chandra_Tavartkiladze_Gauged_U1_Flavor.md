## Abstract

We present a class of models based on a gauged U(1)_F flavor symmetry that explains the hierarchical structure of fermion masses and mixings via the Froggatt-Nielsen (FN) mechanism, while also solving the strong CP problem by the Peccei-Quinn (PQ) mechanism. A global U(1)_PQ symmetry with a nonzero QCD anomaly emerges accidentally in this setup as a byproduct of the gauged U(1)_F symmetry. The resulting axion is shown to be of high quality, with the axion potential safeguarded against quantum gravity corrections by the gauge symmetry. Three models, which are generalizations of the DFSZ axion model, are presented realizing this idea. The right-handed neutrino mass scale is identified as the Froggatt-Nielsen scale in these models. We present explicit UV completions of the FN sectors of these models and show that they preserve the high quality of the axion. In these models, the axion acts as a flavon field, leading to testable predictions in flavor-changing decays of neutral mesons. The axion also serves as the dark matter of the universe with the right amount of relic abundance without causing cosmological domain wall problems. Baryon asymmetry of the universe is realized via leptogenesis which is calculable in these models and found to be of the right order of magnitude.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### 1. General Setup (Section 2)

A gauged U(1)_F flavor symmetry distinguishes generations. Fermion masses arise as powers of a small parameter epsilon = <X>/Lambda_FN through the FN mechanism. Two Higgs doublets are used. Anomaly cancellation conditions constrain the U(1)_F charge assignments.

### 2. Accidental PQ Symmetry (Section 2.2)

The gauged U(1)_F symmetry leads to an accidental global U(1)_PQ with nonzero QCD anomaly. This provides the Peccei-Quinn mechanism for solving the strong CP problem. The axion potential is protected from quantum gravity corrections by the gauge symmetry (high quality axion).

### 3. Three Models

- **Model I** (Section 3): Three right-handed neutrinos with M_R ~ Lambda_FN. Resonant leptogenesis viable. Explicit UV completion preserves axion quality.
- **Model II** (Section 4): High-scale flavor symmetry. Standard thermal leptogenesis.
- **Model III** (Section 5): SU(5)-compatible flavor model. GUT embedding possible.

### 4. Axion Phenomenology (Section 6)

The axion has flavor-violating couplings (acts as a flavon), leading to testable predictions in K -> pi a, B -> K a, and similar decays. Axion-matter couplings can be enhanced or suppressed relative to standard DFSZ models depending on charge assignments.

### 5. Unified Framework

The paper addresses four BSM puzzles simultaneously: (i) fermion mass hierarchy, (ii) strong CP problem, (iii) dark matter, (iv) baryon asymmetry — all from a single gauged U(1)_F symmetry.

## Key Results

1. Gauged U(1)_F flavor symmetry leads to an accidental high-quality PQ symmetry.
2. The axion is protected from quantum gravity corrections by the gauge symmetry.
3. Three explicit DFSZ-type models with UV completions presented.
4. Right-handed neutrino mass scale = Froggatt-Nielsen scale.
5. Axion serves as dark matter with correct relic abundance; no domain wall problem.
6. Leptogenesis (resonant or thermal) produces correct baryon asymmetry.
7. Flavor-violating axion couplings provide testable signatures in meson decays.
