## Abstract

When gravity is quantum, the point structure of space-time should be replaced by a non-commutative geometry. This is true even for quantum gravity in the infra-red. Using the octonions as space-time coordinates, we construct a pre-spacetime, pre-quantum Lagrangian dynamics. We show that the symmetries of this non-commutative space unify the standard model of particle physics with SU(2)_R chiral gravity. The algebra of the octonionic space yields spinor states which can be identified with three generations of quarks and leptons. The geometry of the space implies quantisation of electric charge, and leads to a theoretical derivation of the mysterious mass ratios of quarks and the charged leptons. Quantum gravity is quantisation not only of the gravitational field, but also of the point structure of space-time.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### 1. Quantum Gravity in the Infra-Red (Section I)

The paper argues that quantum gravity is relevant even at low energies when all subsystems are quantum (criterion iii: every subsystem has action ~ hbar). In this regime, the point structure of spacetime is lost even though energy scales are far below Planck scale. Three energy-independent criteria for when gravity is quantum: (i) time scales ~ t_P; (ii) length scales ~ L_P; (iii) every subsystem is quantum.

### 2. Trace Dynamics (Section I)

Building on Adler's pre-quantum theory (trace dynamics), configuration variables and momenta are matrices. The Lagrangian is the trace of a matrix polynomial. A conserved Noether charge C-tilde = sum_i [q_i, p_i] exists from global unitary invariance. Coarse-graining over Planck time scales yields quantum field theory as the emergent dynamics.

### 3. Octonionic Non-Commutative Geometry (Section II)

Minkowski spacetime is replaced by an 8D octonionic space. The four-vector is generalized using octonion units (e_0 through e_7) as space-time coordinates. The line element uses an octonionic metric. The pre-quantum, pre-spacetime action is:

S = (c^4 / 16pi G) integral d^4x sqrt(-g) R + sum_i (q_i-dot / L_P)^2 alpha_i

where alpha_i are Grassmann-valued matrix elements.

### 4. Standard Model Unification

The symmetries of the octonionic space yield:
- SU(3)_c x U(1)_em from split complex octonions
- SU(2)_L x U(1)_Y from the full algebra
- Three generations from the algebraic structure
- Electric charge quantization from the geometry

### 5. Mass Ratios

The geometry of the octonionic space, combined with the algebraic structure, leads to theoretical predictions for mass ratios of quarks and charged leptons. The key parameter is the low-energy fine structure constant alpha_f = e^2/(hbar c) ~ 1/137, which is argued to be quantum gravitational in origin (order unity in Planck units).

Mass ratios m/m_P ~ epsilon << 1 are not individually quantum gravitational, but their ratios are, being determined by the algebraic structure of the octonionic geometry.

## Key Results

1. Quantum gravity is relevant at low energies when all subsystems are quantum (infra-red quantum gravity).
2. Octonionic non-commutative geometry replaces the point structure of spacetime.
3. The Standard Model gauge group SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) with three generations emerges from the algebra of octonions.
4. Electric charge quantization follows from the geometry.
5. Mass ratios of quarks and charged leptons are derived from the octonionic algebraic structure.
6. The fine structure constant is quantum gravitational in origin (order unity in Planck units).
