## Abstract

It is demonstrated how a set of particle representations, familiar from the Standard Model, collectively form a superalgebra. Those representations mirroring the behaviour of the Standard Model's gauge bosons, and three generations of fermions, are each included in this algebra, with exception only to those irreps involving the top quark. This superalgebra is isomorphic to the Euclidean Jordan algebra of 16x16 hermitian matrices, H_16(C), and is generated by division algebras. The division algebraic substructure enables a natural factorization between internal and spacetime symmetries. It also allows for the definition of a Z^5_2 grading on the algebra. Those internal symmetries respecting this substructure are found to be su(3)_C + su(2)_L + u(1)_Y, in addition to four iterations of u(1). For spatial symmetries, one finds multiple copies of so(3). Given its Jordan algebraic foundation, and its apparent non-relativistic character, the model may supply a bridge between particle physics and quantum computing. We close by describing how this article fits into the larger picture of Bott Periodic Particle Physics, first introduced in earlier works.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### 1. Counting Degrees of Freedom

The total number of local off-shell degrees of freedom in the SM (with three generations of right-handed neutrinos) is approximately 250_R. Mathematical structures of this size include:
- The exceptional Lie algebra e_8: 248_R
- The Clifford algebra Cl(0,8) of Bott Periodicity: 256_R
- The hermitian matrices H_16(C): 256_R

### 2. H_16(C) from Division Algebras

H_16(C) is generated by the left action of the algebra C tensor H tensor O on itself. Here C, H, O are the complex numbers, quaternions, and octonions respectively. This is a 256_R-dimensional real algebra.

### 3. Z^5_2 Grading

The division algebraic substructure of H_16(C) enables a Z^5_2 grading (five independent Z_2 gradings simultaneously). This grading produces:
- A (64 + 3*64)_R partitioning of the 256_R-dimensional H_16(C)
- The 64_R piece corresponds to gauge boson representations
- Each of the three 64_R pieces corresponds to one generation of fermions

### 4. Symmetry Content

Internal symmetries respecting the Z^5_2 grading are:
- su(3)_C + su(2)_L + u(1)_Y (the Standard Model gauge algebra)
- Plus four additional u(1) factors
Spatial symmetries yield multiple copies of so(3).

### 5. Fermion-Boson Ratio

The SM fermion-to-boson degree-of-freedom ratio is approximately 3:1. If one recasts the third generation of fermions as a "naively bosonic" product ~Psi*Psi^dagger, this ratio becomes 1:1 — consistent with a superalgebra structure.

### 6. Top Quark Exclusion and Compositeness

All SM irreps are included in H_16(C) except those involving the top quark. This suggests the top quark may be fully composite, while the bottom quark is partially composite. The third generation states may be introduced via a product reminiscent of the spinor-helicity formalism.

### 7. Connection to Bott Periodicity

The model is part of "Bott Periodic Particle Physics": the Clifford algebra Cl(0,8) (period 8 in Bott periodicity) provides the algebraic scaffold. H_16(C) is a hermitian slice of the complexified Cl(8), connecting to the AZ classification (class BDI has Bott period 8).
