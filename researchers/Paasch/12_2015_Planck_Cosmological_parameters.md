# Planck 2015 Results. XIII. Cosmological Parameters

**Author(s):** Planck Collaboration (P. A. R. Ade, N. Aghanim, M. Arnaud, et al.)
**Year:** 2016 (submitted 2015)
**Journal:** Astronomy & Astrophysics 594, A13 (2016)
**arXiv:** 1502.01589
**Relevance:** HIGH

---

## Abstract

This paper presents cosmological results from the full-mission Planck measurements of the CMB anisotropies. We find that the Planck spectra at high multipoles are extremely well described by the standard spatially-flat six-parameter LCDM cosmology with a power-law spectrum of adiabatic scalar perturbations. The Planck data are in very good agreement with the simplest inflationary models. The bestfit LCDM parameters are well determined, with several being measured to sub-percent precision. We also present constraints on one-parameter extensions to the LCDM model, exploring dark energy, neutrino physics, primordial nucleosynthesis, and deviations from standard recombination.

---

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### 1. Six-Parameter LCDM Model

The baseline model assumes spatial flatness (Omega_k = 0), adiabatic scalar perturbations with a power-law spectrum, a cosmological constant (w = -1), standard neutrino physics (N_eff = 3.046, sum m_nu = 0.06 eV), and standard recombination. The six free parameters are: Omega_b h^2, Omega_c h^2, 100*theta_MC, tau, ln(10^{10} A_s), n_s.

### 2. Key Cosmological Parameter Constraints (Planck TT+lowP)

The paper reports best-fit parameters from the Planck temperature power spectrum combined with low-multipole polarization data.

### 3. Extensions Beyond LCDM

- Dark energy equation of state: w = -1.019 (+0.075/-0.080) (Planck TT+lowP+lensing+ext), consistent with cosmological constant
- Curvature: Omega_k = 0.000 +/- 0.005
- Neutrino effective number: N_eff = 3.13 +/- 0.32
- Neutrino mass sum: sum m_nu < 0.23 eV (95% CL)
- Running of spectral index: dn_s/d ln k = -0.008 +/- 0.008

## Key Results Summary

1. LCDM provides an excellent fit to the Planck data with six parameters
2. H_0 = 67.27 +/- 0.66 km/s/Mpc — in 3.4 sigma tension with local distance-ladder measurements (Riess et al. H_0 = 73.24)
3. Omega_m = 0.3156 +/- 0.0091, Omega_Lambda = 0.6844 +/- 0.0091
4. n_s = 0.9645 +/- 0.0049 — excludes exact Harrison-Zel'dovich (n_s = 1) at > 5 sigma
5. tau = 0.079 +/- 0.017 (optical depth to reionization)
6. sigma_8 = 0.831 +/- 0.013 (amplitude of matter fluctuations)
7. Dark energy equation of state consistent with w = -1 (cosmological constant)
8. No evidence for spatial curvature: Omega_k = 0.000 +/- 0.005
9. No evidence for extra neutrino species: N_eff = 3.13 +/- 0.32
10. Neutrino mass bound: sum m_nu < 0.23 eV (95% CL)
