## Abstract

According to conformal cyclic cosmology (CCC), the currently conventional description of the entire history of the universe (but without an initial inflationary phase) provides but one cosmic aeon of an unending sequence of such aeons, where the future conformal infinity of each aeon joins essentially smoothly to the conformally stretched big bang of the next, across a spacelike 3-surface, referred to as a crossover 3-surface. Whereas in previous accounts of CCC a detailed description of the physics of crossover had been somewhat problematic, a novel idea is introduced here to show how crossover takes place naturally during a temporal period of the universe that is dominated by gravitational waves referred to here as a gravitational wave epoch (GWE). Accordingly, the geometry at the crossover surface is conformally smooth, except at a discrete set of points, referred to as Hawking points, each representing the final Hawking evaporation of the dominant black hole of a galactic cluster in the earlier aeon. It is shown here (using 2-spinor and twistor techniques) that there is a mass-energy conservation law that holds across the crossover surface, showing that the rise of temperature within such Hawking spots should be effectively determined by the total mass of the pre-crossover galactic cluster involved. This rise of temperature on the CMB map within Hawking spots is found to be in quantitative agreement with the masses of the largest galactic clusters observed in our own aeon what suggests that the physics in the previous aeon was, at least in the gravitational sector, similar to ours. A second observational feature, the actual angular diameter of the Hawking spots seen in our CMB, which is about twice what should have been expected, is associated to the presence of GWE just after the crossover and before the start of the usual cosmological epochs.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

The paper develops the physics of crossover between aeons in Conformal Cyclic Cosmology (CCC) using 2-spinor and twistor methods. The authors label the current aeon A and the previous aeon B, joined across a spacelike 3-surface X (crossover 3-surface); the full space-time is M = A ∪ X ∪ B. Each aeon's future conformal infinity becomes the conformally stretched big bang of the next, assuming positive cosmological constant Λ.

Section II introduces the 2-spinor formalism: the Weyl tensor decomposes as C_abcd = Ψ_ABCD ε_A'B' ε_C'D' + c.c. with totally symmetric Ψ_ABCD. In vacuum with Λ, the Bianchi identity gives the spin-2 massless wave equation ∇^{AA'} Ψ_ABCD = 0. Crucially, they introduce ψ_ABCD equal to Ψ_ABCD in the physical metric but conformally rescaled as ψ → ω^{-1} Ψ under g → ω^2 g, so ψ has conformal weight -1 and the propagation equation ∇^{AA'} ψ_ABCD = 0 is conformally invariant. This ψ rather than Ψ is what carries information across the crossover (following Friedrich's result that in generic expanding models Ψ vanishes at I+, so the relevant quantity is the derivative, equivalent to ψ at I+).

Section III establishes the mass conservation law. A twistor ω^A satisfies ∇^{(A}_{A'} ω^{B)} = 0; the solution space is the 4-dimensional twistor space T. A symmetric rank-2 twistor (Killing spinor) κ^{AB} satisfies ∇^{(A}_{A'} κ^{BC)} = 0; the solution space is 10-dimensional. Contracting ψ_ABCD with κ^{CD} gives φ_AB = ψ_ABCD κ^{CD} satisfying the free Maxwell equation ∇^{AA'} φ_AB = 0. The closed 2-form p_ab = φ_AB ε_A'B' can be integrated over a closed 2-surface S surrounding the Hawking point's world-line h to give a conserved charge, which for the spherically symmetric κ^{AB} = x^{(A}_{C'} t^{B)C'} evaluates in the Schwarzschild case (ψ = -(6mG/r^3) o_(A o_B i_C i_D)) to M = 4πmG. This conservation law forces the entire pre-crossover galactic cluster mass to be injected into the succeeding aeon at the Hawking point H.

Section IV applies this: taking M_gc ~ 10^15 M_sun for the largest galaxy clusters and ρ_LS ~ 1.2·10^{-20} kg/m^3 at last scattering, the injected density within a Hawking spot is δρ ~ 10^{-22} kg/m^3, giving δT/T ~ δρ/(4ρ_LS) ~ 10^{-3}, in agreement with observed Hawking-spot temperature excesses.

Section V unifies the standard cosmological epochs (RDE, MDE, LDE) using a Weierstrass-P scale-factor solution a(η) = (P - A/(2B^2) P')/(P^2 - A^2 H_Λ^2/(4B^4)) with g_2 = A^2 H_Λ^2/B^4 and g_3 = -H_Λ^2/B^2. Section VI introduces the Gravitational Wave Epoch (GWE) as a pre-crossover epoch where C >> E, S (Weyl dominates Ricci), with a Weyl-gas emerging from black-hole evaporation, dark-matter ("erebon") decay, or the Cosmic Gravitational Background. A linearized spherical gravitational wave in de Sitter obeys F'' + F(k^2 - 8/(η_∞ - η)^2) = 0 with Bessel-function solutions. The onset of GWE occurs at η_∞ - η_GW < 10 s, corresponding to cosmic time t_GW - t_0 ~ 2·10^{19} s ~ 10^3 billion years. Section VII argues CCC requires negative spatial curvature (only very slightly, consistent with Planck) to keep future light cones expanding through successive aeons. Section VIII estimates that cluster magnetic fields contribute δT_B/T ~ 10^{-10} to Hawking-spot B-modes. Section IX matches the Grand Arc and Big Ring structures to black-hole collision times ~2-5× the age of our universe in the previous aeon.

## Key Results

1. A conserved mass invariant across the CCC crossover is derived from twistor theory: integrating φ_AB ε_A'B' over a sphere S surrounding a Hawking point gives M = 4πmG where m is the mass of the pre-crossover galactic cluster.
2. Predicted Hawking-spot CMB temperature excess δT/T ~ 10^{-3} for M_gc ~ 10^15 M_sun, quantitatively matching the observational value reported in the An, Meissner, Nurowski, Penrose (MNRAS 2020) analysis.
3. A novel Gravitational Wave Epoch (GWE) is proposed to straddle the crossover, explaining the anomalously large angular diameter (~0.04 rad) of observed Hawking spots via an extra conformal-time interval η^G ~ 2·10^{16} s that widens the causal region between crossover and last scattering.
4. CCC is argued to require negative spatial curvature for the iterated-aeon future-light-cone argument to work.
5. Large-scale structures (Grand Arc ~1 Gpc, Big Ring ~200 Mpc) can be interpreted as imprints of black-hole collisions occurring at conformal times t_col ~ 23-70 billion years in the preceding aeon.

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

The paper is oppositional to the phonon-exflation picture in its explanatory direction: CCC keeps a classical Weyl-dominated gravitational gas as the fundamental substrate at crossover, whereas the phonon-exflation framework would interpret the Weyl tensor components C_abcd as arising from spectral moments of D_K on Jensen-deformed SU(3), i.e., emergent from the fiber rather than fundamental. Still, a few structural parallels are worth noting: (i) the Weyl-dominated epoch before crossover resembles a regime where the a_2 Seeley-DeWitt coefficient (which generates Einstein-Hilbert) is suppressed relative to higher spectral moments, and the CCC picture of the crossover as a conformally smooth transition with point-like singularities could be compared to the fold at tau ~ 0.19 where the substrate's spectral content reorganizes. (ii) The mass-conservation theorem proved via Killing-spinor contraction is a structural result about twistor geometry that would survive translation into the spectral-triple language and could constrain how spectral weight crosses a first-order transition. (iii) The required negative spatial curvature is at odds with the near-flat FLRW assumed in most phonon-exflation computations. Overall this is a GR-first paper whose framing the phonon-exflation substrate view would need to invert.
