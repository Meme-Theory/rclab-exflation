## Topic Map

### A. Dark Matter Annihilation and Energy Injection
Papers: 01, 04, 17
Mack's core program: DM annihilation power across cosmic time, circumgalactic medium heating at z=20-40, Jeans mass suppression in minihalos. Paper 01 establishes the framework with density-squared scaling; Paper 04 applies it to CGM ionization; Paper 17 adds velocity offsets and 21cm predictions.

### B. Dark Matter Models and Direct Detection
Papers: 06, 08, 10, 15, 16, 18
Comprehensive coverage of DM candidates (WIMPs, PBHs, ultralight bosons, hidden sectors) and detection strategies (directional TPC, gravitational waves, neutron star heating). Paper 10 is a TASI review spanning 40 orders of magnitude in DM mass.

### C. Extra Dimensions and Microscopic Black Holes
Papers: 05, 11, 13
LED models with lowered Planck scale producing micro black holes. Paper 05 constrains via vacuum decay + UHECR; Paper 11 identifies neutrino telescope signatures; Paper 13 derives PBH dark matter in LED scenarios with 2-6 extra dimensions.

### D. Hubble Tension and Cosmological Parameters
Papers: 02, 07, 12, 29, 30
Systematic analysis of H0 tension in 2D parameter space (H0-Omega_m), SN Ia extinction systematics, uncalibrated cosmic standards insensitive to early-universe physics. Planck and DESI provide precision benchmarks.

### E. Greene: Compactification, Moduli, and Inflation
Papers: 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24
Greene's program on KK geometry in cosmology: Casimir stabilization of extra dimensions (19), Brandenberger-Vafa 3D selection (20), large-gap hyperbolic compactifications with bulk inflaton (21), brane motion splitting KK tower (22), landscape instabilities in high-dimensional moduli (23), computational complexity measure (24).

### F. Non-Orientable Geometry and CP Violation
Papers: 25, 26
Klein bottle compactification producing CP violation from topology alone. Fermion condensate walls at parity fixed points; Bogoliubov particle production as brane transits wall; baryogenesis scenario meeting all Sakharov conditions.

### G. Dark Energy, Vacuum Decay, and Inflation
Papers: 09, 27, 28
Foundational reviews: dark energy phenomenology (09), vacuum metastability and bounce solutions (27), asymptotic safety inflation from NGFP (28). Paper 09 is the canonical Frieman-Turner-Huterer review.

### H. Observational Facilities and Methods
Papers: 03, 08, 14
SKA 21cm cosmic dawn (03), CYGNUS directional detector (08), SkyHopper GRB nano-satellite (14). Instruments probing high-z universe and DM signatures.

### Paper 01: Known Unknowns of Dark Matter Annihilation Over Cosmic Time
- **File**: `01_2013_Mack_DM_Annihilation_Unknowns.md`
- **arXiv**: 1309.7783
- **Year**: 2013
- **Relevance**: HIGH
- **Tags**: dark matter, annihilation, halo structure, mass-concentration, cosmic time

**Summary**: Mack quantifies the annihilation power from DM halos across cosmic history, separating smooth and structure components. The annihilation rate scales as rho_DM^2, making predictions extremely sensitive to halo density profiles (NFW, Einasto, Burkert), minimum mass cutoff (10^{-11} to 10^7 M_sun), and mass-concentration relations. Predictions span multiple orders of magnitude. Peak annihilation occurs at z~10-40.

**Key Results**:
- Structure component dominates over smooth at late times by ~4 orders of magnitude
- Minimum mass cutoff is the dominant source of uncertainty
- Mass-concentration relation varies predictions by 2-4 orders of magnitude
- Sweet spot for observing annihilation: z~20-40
