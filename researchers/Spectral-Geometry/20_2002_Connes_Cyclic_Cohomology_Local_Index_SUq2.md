## Abstract

We analyse the NC-space underlying the quantum group SU_q(2) from the spectral point of view which is the basis of noncommutative geometry, and show how the general theory developed in our joint work with H. Moscovici applies to the specific spectral triple defined by Chakraborty and Pal. This provides the pseudo-differential calculus, the Wodzicki-type residue, and the local cyclic cocycle giving the index formula. The cochain whose coboundary is the difference between the original Chern character and the local one is given by the remainders in the rational approximation of the logarithmic derivative of the Dedekind eta function. This specific example allows to illustrate the general notion of locality in NCG. The formulas computing the residue are "local". Locality by stripping all the expressions from irrelevant details makes them computable. The key feature of this spectral triple is its equivariance, i.e. the SU_q(2)-symmetry. We shall explain how this leads naturally to the general concept of invariant cyclic cohomology in the framework of quantum group symmetries.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### 1. Spectral Triple for SU_q(2)

The algebra A is the algebra of functions on SU_q(2) with generators alpha, beta satisfying:

alpha* alpha + beta* beta = 1, alpha alpha* + q^2 beta beta* = 1, alpha beta = q beta alpha, alpha beta* = q beta* alpha, beta beta* = beta* beta

The Hilbert space H has orthonormal basis e^{(n)}_{ij} where n ranges over half-integers and i, j in {-n,...,n}. The Dirac operator is:

D(e^{(n)}_{ij}) = (2 delta_0(n - i) - 1) * 2n * e^{(n)}_{ij}

The representation formulas for alpha and beta in terms of coefficients a_+/-, b_+/- are given explicitly.

### 2. Dimension Spectrum

The dimension spectrum of SU_q(2) (the set of singularities of zeta functions zeta_b(z) = Trace(b|D|^{-z})) is simple and equal to {1, 2, 3} in C. Simplicity means all singularities are at most simple poles. The noncommutative integral is:

integral P = Res_{z=0} Trace(P|D|^{-z})

This defines a trace on the algebra generated by A, [D, A], and |D|^z.

### 3. The Local Index Theorem (Connes-Moscovici)

The general local index formula gives odd components of a (b, B)-cocycle:

phi_n(a_0,...,a_n) = sum_k c_{n,k} integral a_0 [D, a_1]^{(k_1)} ... [D, a_n]^{(k_n)} |D|^{-n-2|k|}

where k is a multi-index, |k| = k_1 + ... + k_n, and c_{n,k} involves Gamma function values. The pairing with K_1(A) gives the Fredholm index.

For SU_q(2) with metric dimension 3, the local index cocycle has two components:

phi_1(a_0, a_1) = integral a_0 [D, a_1]|D|^{-1} - (1/4) integral a_0 nabla([D, a_1])|D|^{-3} + (1/8) integral a_0 nabla^2([D, a_1])|D|^{-5}

phi_3(a_0, a_1, a_2, a_3) = (1/12) integral a_0 [D, a_1][D, a_2][D, a_3]|D|^{-3}

where nabla(T) = [D^2, T].

### 4. The Case q = 0

The degenerate case q = 0 is worked out in full detail. The operators alpha and beta simplify dramatically, and the algebra structure is captured by an exact sequence:

0 -> J -> A -sigma-> C^infty(S^1) -> 0

where J is isomorphic to C^infty(S^1) tensor S (matrices of rapid decay), and sigma captures the "symbol" of the operator.

### 5. Cosphere Bundle and Geodesic Flow

The pseudo-differential calculus on SU_q(2) yields the cosphere bundle S*_q, endowed with a one-parameter group gamma_t of automorphisms (geodesic flow), and related to the product D^2_{q+} x D^2_{q-} of two noncommutative 2-disks. The residue formulas in terms of the symbol rho(b) in C^infty(S*_q) are:

integral b|D|^{-3} = (tau_1 tensor tau_1)(r rho(b)_0)
integral b|D|^{-2} = (tau_1 tensor tau_0 + tau_0 tensor tau_1)(r rho(b)_0)
integral b|D|^{-1} = (tau_0 tensor tau_0)(r rho(b)_0)

where tau_1 is a trace from integration on S^1 and tau_0 is a regularized trace on the ideal.

### 6. Eta-Cochain and Dedekind Eta Function

The cochain whose coboundary gives the difference between the original Chern character and the local one involves the remainders in the rational approximation of the logarithmic derivative of the Dedekind eta function -- a connection between noncommutative geometry and modular forms.

### 7. Quantum Group Invariant Cyclic Cohomology

The equivariance under SU_q(2) of the spectral triple leads to the general concept of invariant cyclic cohomology in the framework of quantum group symmetries.

## Key Results

1. **Dimension spectrum of SU_q(2)**: Simple, equal to {1, 2, 3}.

2. **Local index formula**: Explicit computation of the local cyclic cocycle for SU_q(2).

3. **Cosphere bundle S*_q**: Identified with its geodesic flow and relation to NC 2-disks.

4. **Eta-cochain**: Given by remainders in rational approximation of (log eta)'.

5. **Invariant cyclic cohomology**: New concept from quantum group symmetries.

6. **Locality principle**: "Local" formulas strip irrelevant details, making expressions computable even for quantum groups.

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

This paper demonstrates the local index formula in practice on a quantum group, providing a worked example of how the Connes-Moscovici machinery computes topological invariants from spectral data. For the phonon-exflation framework, the SU_q(2) example is structurally analogous to the project's SU(3) spectral triple: both involve Dirac operators on compact Lie groups (or their deformations), Frobenius decomposition into representation-theoretic components, and explicit computation of the dimension spectrum. The fact that SU_q(2) has dimension spectrum {1, 2, 3} while the project's SU(3) has a richer structure (due to rank 2) is informative. The pseudo-differential calculus and cosphere bundle construction could guide the project's analysis of the spectral action beyond the heat kernel approximation.
