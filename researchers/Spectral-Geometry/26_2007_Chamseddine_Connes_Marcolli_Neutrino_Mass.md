## Abstract

We present an effective unified theory based on noncommutative geometry for the standard model with neutrino mixing, minimally coupled to gravity. The unification is based on the symplectic unitary group in Hilbert space and on the spectral action. It yields all the detailed structure of the standard model with several predictions at unification scale. Besides the familiar predictions for the gauge couplings as for GUT theories, it predicts the Higgs scattering parameter and the sum of the squares of Yukawa couplings. From these relations one can extract predictions at low energy, giving in particular a Higgs mass around 170 GeV and a top mass compatible with present experimental value. The geometric picture that emerges is that space-time is the product of an ordinary spin manifold (for which the theory would deliver Einstein gravity) by a finite noncommutative geometry $F$. The discrete space $F$ is of KO-dimension 6 modulo 8 and of metric dimension 0, and accounts for all the intricacies of the standard model with its spontaneous symmetry breaking Higgs sector.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

**The finite algebra.** The input is the left-right symmetric algebra $A_{LR} = \mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{H}_L \oplus \mathbb{H}_R \oplus M_3(\mathbb{C})$. The natural representation is the sum of irreducible bimodules of odd spin, giving $M_F$ of dimension 32 per generation (Proposition 2.4). The bimodule $M_F = E \oplus E^0$ with $E = 2_L \otimes 1^0 \oplus 2_R \otimes 1^0 \oplus 2_L \otimes 3^0 \oplus 2_R \otimes 3^0$.

**Subalgebra selection.** Geometric considerations (existence of off-diagonal Dirac operators satisfying the order one condition) select a unique maximal subalgebra $A_F = \mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{H} \oplus M_3(\mathbb{C})$ (Proposition 2.11). This is the algebra of the Standard Model.

**KO-dimension 6.** The real structure $J_F$ satisfies $J_F^2 = 1$, $J_F \gamma_F = -\gamma_F J_F$, characteristic of KO-dimension 6 mod 8. This allows simultaneous Weyl-Majorana condition, solving the fermion doubling problem.

**Gauge symmetries.** The group $SU(A_F) \cong U(1) \times SU(2) \times SU(3)$ up to finite abelian groups (Proposition 2.16). The U(1) hypercharge assignments are correctly reproduced (table 2.27).

**Classification of Dirac operators.** All Dirac operators satisfying the axioms depend on 31 real parameters: the Yukawa coupling matrices $Y_{(\uparrow 1)}, Y_{(\downarrow 1)}, Y_{(\uparrow 3)}, Y_{(\downarrow 3)}$ and the symmetric Majorana mass matrix $Y_R$ (Theorem 2.21). These encode quark/lepton masses, CKM and PMNS angles, and Majorana masses.

**Spectral action.** The action functional $\text{Tr}(f(D/\Lambda)) + \frac{1}{2}\langle J\psi, D\psi \rangle$ applied to the product geometry $M \times F$ yields the full Standard Model Lagrangian minimally coupled to gravity. The bosonic part gives Einstein-Hilbert + Weyl curvature squared + Higgs sector. The fermionic part gives all correct couplings.

**Predictions.** Three predictions under the "big desert" hypothesis: (1) GUT-type coupling unification $g_2 = g_3 = \sqrt{5/3}\, g_1$; (2) Higgs scattering parameter at unification; (3) mass relation $\sum_{\text{gen}} (m_e^2 + m_\nu^2 + 3m_d^2 + 3m_u^2) = 8M_W^2$.

## Key Results

1. **KO-dimension 6**: The finite geometry $F$ has metric dimension 0 but KO-dimension 6 mod 8
2. **Unique algebra**: $\mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{H} \oplus M_3(\mathbb{C})$ is the unique maximal subalgebra admitting off-diagonal Dirac operators
3. **31 parameters**: Complete classification of all Dirac operators on the finite geometry
4. **SM Lagrangian**: Full derivation of the Standard Model Lagrangian from the spectral action
5. **See-saw mechanism**: Neutrino mixing with Majorana mass terms arises naturally
6. **Fermion-boson mass relation**: $\sum(m_e^2 + m_\nu^2 + 3m_d^2 + 3m_u^2) = 8M_W^2$ at unification
