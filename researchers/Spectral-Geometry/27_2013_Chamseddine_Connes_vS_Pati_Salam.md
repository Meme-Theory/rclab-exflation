# Beyond the Spectral Standard Model: Emergence of Pati-Salam Unification

**Author(s):** Ali H. Chamseddine, Alain Connes, Walter D. van Suijlekom
**Year:** 2013 (revised 2014)
**Journal:** JHEP 1311 (2013) 132
**arXiv:** 1304.8050
**Relevance:** MEDIUM

---

## Abstract

The assumption that space-time is a noncommutative space formed as a product of a continuous four dimensional manifold times a finite space predicts, almost uniquely, the Standard Model with all its fermions, gauge fields, Higgs field and their representations. A strong restriction on the noncommutative space results from the first order condition which came from the requirement that the Dirac operator is a differential operator of order one. Without this restriction, invariance under inner automorphisms requires the inner fluctuations of the Dirac operator to contain a quadratic piece expressed in terms of the linear part. We apply the classification of product noncommutative spaces without the first order condition and show that this leads immediately to a Pati-Salam $SU(2)_R \times SU(2)_L \times SU(4)$ type model which unifies leptons and quarks in four colors.

---

## Key Arguments and Derivations

**Relaxing the first order condition.** In the standard NCG approach, the first order condition $[[D, a], b^0] = 0$ (with $b^0 = Jb^*J^{-1}$) restricts the algebra from $\mathbb{H}_R \oplus \mathbb{H}_L \oplus M_4(\mathbb{C})$ to the SM subalgebra $\mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{H} \oplus M_3(\mathbb{C})$. When this condition is dropped (motivated by quantum groups and quantum spheres where it fails), the full Pati-Salam algebra $A = \mathbb{H}_R \oplus \mathbb{H}_L \oplus M_4(\mathbb{C})$ becomes available.

**Non-linear inner fluctuations.** Without the first order condition, the inner fluctuations acquire a quadratic correction: $D' = D + A^{(1)} + \tilde{A}^{(1)} + A^{(2)}$, where $A^{(2)} = \sum_j \hat{a}_j [A^{(1)}, \hat{b}_j]$ depends quadratically on $A^{(1)}$. These form a semigroup $\text{Pert}(A) \subset A \otimes A^{op}$.

**Pati-Salam gauge symmetry.** The inner automorphisms of $A = \mathbb{H}_R \oplus \mathbb{H}_L \oplus M_4(\mathbb{C})$ yield the Pati-Salam gauge group $SU(2)_R \times SU(2)_L \times SU(4)$, with $SU(4)$ unifying color and lepton number.

**Higgs field content.** Fundamental Higgs fields in representations $(2, 2, 1)$, $(2, 1, 4)$, and $(1, 1, 1+15)$ of $SU(2)_R \times SU(2)_L \times SU(4)$. When the initial Dirac operator satisfies the first order condition relative to $\mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{H} \oplus M_3(\mathbb{C})$, additional Higgs fields from $A^{(2)}$ are composite (quadratic in fundamental ones). For generic initial Dirac operators, all are fundamental.

**Tensorial notation.** A systematic tensorial notation is developed where the 384x384 matrix representation is decomposed as $A = \alpha I$ with $\alpha$ acted on by quaternionic matrices and $I$ by $M_4(\mathbb{C})$. The Dirac action takes the form $\Psi^*_M D^N_M \Psi_N$.

**Spectral action computation.** Using a cutoff function and heat kernel expansion, the resultant model is the full Pati-Salam model with all Higgs fields necessary to break the symmetry down to $U(1)_{\text{em}} \times SU(3)_c$.

## Key Results

1. Dropping the first order condition uniquely leads to the Pati-Salam algebra $\mathbb{H}_R \oplus \mathbb{H}_L \oplus M_4(\mathbb{C})$
2. Inner fluctuations acquire quadratic corrections forming a semigroup $\text{Pert}(A)$
3. 16 fundamental fermions in $(2, 1, 4) + (1, 2, 4)$ representation
4. Fundamental Higgs in $(2, 2, 1)$, $(2, 1, 4)$, $(1, 1, 1+15)$ representations
5. Model truncates correctly to the Standard Model
6. Composite Higgs fields from $A^{(2)}$ break Pati-Salam to SM at high energies

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

The Pati-Salam extension is directly relevant as the natural UV completion of the M4 x SU(3) framework. The project's analysis of the fiber geometry uses the algebra $\mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{H} \oplus M_3(\mathbb{C})$ (the SM subalgebra), but the full algebra $\mathbb{H}_R \oplus \mathbb{H}_L \oplus M_4(\mathbb{C})$ emerges when the first order condition is relaxed -- precisely the situation expected at early times ($\tau \to 0$) before symmetry breaking. The quadratic inner fluctuations $A^{(2)}$ provide additional Higgs fields that could drive the transition from Pati-Salam to SM symmetry, potentially connecting to the exflation dynamics.
