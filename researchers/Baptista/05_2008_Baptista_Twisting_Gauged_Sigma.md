# Twisting gauged non-linear sigma-models

**Author(s):** J. M. Baptista
**Year:** 2007 (published 2008)
**Journal:** JHEP 0802 (2008) 096
**arXiv:** 0707.2786
**Relevance:** LOW (vortex foundations, not directly used in KK program)

---

## Abstract

We consider gauged sigma-models from a Riemann surface into a Kahler and hamiltonian G-manifold X. The supersymmetric N=2 theory can always be twisted to produce a gauged A-model. This model localizes to the moduli space of solutions of the vortex equations and computes the Hamiltonian Gromov-Witten invariants. When the target is equivariantly Calabi-Yau, i.e. when its first G-equivariant Chern class vanishes, the supersymmetric theory can also be twisted into a gauged B-model. This model localizes to the Kahler quotient X//G.

---

## Key Results

1. **Gauged A-model**: The N=2 gauged nonlinear sigma model on a Riemann surface with Kahler hamiltonian G-manifold target X can always be twisted along the vector R-symmetry to produce a gauged A-model. This model localizes to the moduli space of vortex equations and its correlation functions compute Hamiltonian Gromov-Witten invariants.

2. **Gauged B-model**: When the target X is equivariantly Calabi-Yau (first G-equivariant Chern class c_1^G(X) = 0), the N=2 theory can also be twisted along the axial R-symmetry to produce a gauged B-model. This model localizes to the Kahler quotient X//G.

3. **R-symmetry anomaly analysis**: The vector R-symmetry is always anomaly-free in the gauged model (just as in the ungauged case). The axial R-symmetry is anomaly-free precisely when c_1^G(X) = 0, which is the equivariant Calabi-Yau condition.

4. **Observables**: The gauged A-model observables are elements of the G-equivariant cohomology H_G^*(X), while the gauged B-model observables are related to the cohomology of the quotient X//G.

5. **Adiabatic limit**: In the limit e^2 -> infinity (strong gauge coupling), the vortex equations reduce to holomorphic curve equations in X//G, connecting gauged A-model invariants to ordinary GW invariants of the quotient.

6. **Gauged Calabi-Yau/Landau-Ginzburg correspondence**: The gauged B-model is related via mirror symmetry to gauged Landau-Ginzburg models, extending the classical Calabi-Yau/LG correspondence to the equivariant setting.

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

This paper provides the full topological field theory treatment of gauged nonlinear sigma models, constructing both A and B twists. The equivariantly Calabi-Yau condition for the B-twist existence and the localization to the Kahler quotient X//G are relevant mathematical structures. The gauged B-model's localization to X//G connects to the dimensional reduction picture where higher-dimensional gauge theory on a product manifold reduces to a sigma model with target being the internal space quotient -- a key idea in Baptista's later Kaluza-Klein work.
