# A topological gauged sigma-model

**Author(s):** J. M. Baptista
**Year:** 2005
**Journal:** Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. 9, 1007 (2005)
**arXiv:** hep-th/0502152
**Relevance:** LOW (vortex foundations, not directly used in KK program)

---

## Abstract

We describe a topological field theory that studies the moduli space of solutions of the symplectic vortex equations. It contains as special cases the topological sigma-model and topological Yang-Mills over Kahler surfaces. The correlation functions of the theory are closely related to the recently introduced Hamiltonian Gromov-Witten invariants.

---

## Key Results

1. **Topological field theory for vortex equations**: A cohomological topological field theory is constructed whose path integral localizes on the moduli space of solutions to the symplectic vortex equations on a gauged nonlinear sigma model with Kahler domain M and Kahler target X.

2. **Unification of topological sigma-model and topological Yang-Mills**: When the gauge group G is trivial, the theory reduces to the topological sigma-model (A-model). When X is a point and M is four-dimensional, it reduces to topological Yang-Mills over Kahler surfaces.

3. **Vortex equations**: The energy functional E(A,phi) = integral of ||F_A||^2 + ||d_A phi||^2 + ||mu o phi||^2 is minimized by solutions of the first-order equations: (i) d-bar_A phi = 0, (ii) Lambda F_A + e^2 mu o phi = 0, (iii) F_A^{0,2} = 0.

4. **Observable construction**: Natural observables are defined as closed elements of the G-equivariant cohomology complex of the space of fields A x Gamma(E), and are related to "universal" constructions generalizing those from topological Yang-Mills and the topological sigma-model.

5. **Correlation functions and Hamiltonian GW invariants**: After localization, correlation functions are expressed as integrals over the finite-dimensional vortex moduli space, closely related to the Hamiltonian Gromov-Witten invariants of Cieliebak-Gaio-Mundet-Salamon.

6. **Torus action moduli space structure**: For abelian gauge group (torus actions), the moduli space of vortex solutions has a concrete description, and the virtual dimension is computed.

7. **Adiabatic limit**: In the limit e -> infinity, the vortex equations reduce to the holomorphic curve equations in the symplectic quotient X//G, connecting vortex invariants to ordinary Gromov-Witten invariants.

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

This paper constructs the topological field theory framework for gauged sigma models, unifying the topological sigma-model and topological Yang-Mills. The symplectic vortex equations and their moduli spaces provide the mathematical language for studying BPS configurations in gauged field theories on Kahler manifolds. This is part of the technical infrastructure Baptista develops before turning to Kaluza-Klein reductions, where similar gauged sigma models arise from dimensional reduction of higher-dimensional gauge theories over compact internal spaces.
