# Vortex equations in abelian gauged sigma-models

**Author(s):** J. M. Baptista
**Year:** 2004 (published 2006)
**Journal:** Commun. Math. Phys. 261, 161 (2006)
**arXiv:** math/0411517
**Relevance:** LOW (vortex foundations, not directly used in KK program)

---

## Abstract

We consider nonlinear gauged sigma-models with Kahler domain and target. For a special choice of potential these models admit Bogomolny (or self-duality) equations -- the so-called vortex equations. Here we describe the space of solutions and energy spectrum of the vortex equations when the gauge group is a torus T^n, the domain is compact, and the target is C^n or CP^n. We also obtain a large family of solutions when the target is a compact Kahler toric manifold.

---

## Key Results

1. **Vortex equations for nonlinear gauged sigma models**: For Kahler domain M and Kahler target F with hamiltonian G-action and moment map mu, the energy functional E(A,phi) admits Bogomolny equations (vortex equations) when the potential is V(phi) = 2||mu o phi||^2.

2. **Complete solution space for T^n on C^n**: For gauge group T^n acting on F = C^n, the moduli space of vortex solutions is fully described for any compact Kahler base M. This generalizes Bradlow's results for n=1.

3. **Complete solution space for T^n on CP^n**: The space of solutions and energy spectrum of vortex equations are completely determined for G = T^n, F = CP^n, and any compact Kahler base M, generalizing earlier results for Riemann surfaces with n=1.

4. **Complex vs real gauge equivalence**: For torus actions on Kahler toric targets, the stability criterion of Mundet i Riera simplifies dramatically, yielding an almost perfect correspondence between moduli spaces up to real gauge transformations G and complexified gauge transformations G^C.

5. **Moment polytope interplay**: The space of vortex solutions exhibits a deep interplay with the geometry of the moment polytope mu(F) from the torus action on F.

6. **Solutions on quotient targets**: A general procedure for constructing vortex solutions on quotient target manifolds F/H from solutions on F, applied to produce families of solutions when the target is a compact Kahler toric manifold.

7. **Energy spectrum**: The energy of vortex solutions is topological, determined by the homotopy class of the section phi, given by the formula E = T[phi] when the vortex equations are satisfied.

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

This paper provides the detailed mathematical analysis of vortex moduli spaces for abelian gauged sigma models on compact Kahler manifolds, including the role of moment maps and toric geometry. The interplay between moment polytopes and vortex solution spaces is a key ingredient in understanding how gauge-theoretic structures on fiber bundles constrain the topology and geometry of solutions. These techniques feed directly into Baptista's later work on Kaluza-Klein reductions, where the internal compact Kahler manifold plays the role of the sigma-model target.
