# Abelian vortices with singularities

**Author(s):** J. M. Baptista, Indranil Biswas
**Year:** 2012 (published 2013)
**Journal:** Differential Geom. Appl. 31, 725 (2013)
**arXiv:** 1207.0863
**Relevance:** LOW (vortex foundations, not directly used in KK program)

---

## Abstract

Let L -> X be a complex line bundle over a compact connected Riemann surface. We consider the abelian vortex equations on L when the metric on the surface has finitely many point degeneracies or conical singularities and the line bundle has parabolic structure. These conditions appear naturally in the study of vortex configurations with constraints, or configurations invariant under the action of a finite group. We first show that the moduli space of singular vortex solutions is the same as in the regular case. Then we compute the total volume and total scalar curvature of the moduli space of singular vortex solutions. These numbers differ from the case of regular vortices by a very natural term. Finally we exhibit explicit non-trivial vortex solutions over the thrice-punctured hyperbolic sphere.

---

## Key Results

1. **Singular vortex equations**: The abelian vortex equations are studied on a line bundle L -> X over a compact Riemann surface where the background metric has finitely many point degeneracies or conical singularities (omega behaves as |z|^{2 beta_k} with beta_k > -1 at singular points) and the line bundle has parabolic structure.

2. **Moduli space preservation**: Despite the singularities, the moduli space of vortex solutions is the same as in the regular case -- it is the symmetric product Sym^d(X) where d is the effective degree.

3. **Volume and scalar curvature**: The total volume and total scalar curvature of the singular vortex moduli space are computed. They differ from the regular case by natural correction terms involving the singular weights.

4. **Explicit solutions on thrice-punctured sphere**: Non-trivial vortex solutions are exhibited explicitly on the thrice-punctured hyperbolic sphere (genus 0 with 3 punctures), which is conformally the quotient H/Gamma for a Fuchsian group Gamma.

5. **Finite group invariance**: Singular vortex configurations arise naturally when studying vortex configurations invariant under the action of a finite group on the Riemann surface.

6. **Parabolic Hitchin-Kobayashi**: The results rely on the parabolic version of the Hitchin-Kobayashi correspondence, relating stable parabolic bundles to vortex solutions with parabolic singularities.

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

This paper extends vortex theory to singular backgrounds (degenerate metrics and parabolic bundles), showing that the moduli space structure is robust under such singularities. The treatment of conical singularities on the base manifold and the preservation of moduli space structure is relevant to understanding field configurations on spaces with orbifold points -- a common feature of Kaluza-Klein compactifications with discrete symmetries. The finite group invariance connection provides a bridge to orbifold compactifications.
