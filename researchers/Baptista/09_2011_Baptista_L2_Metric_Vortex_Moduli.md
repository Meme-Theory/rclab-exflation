# On the L2-metric of vortex moduli spaces

**Author(s):** J. M. Baptista
**Year:** 2010 (published 2011)
**Journal:** Nucl. Phys. B 844, 308 (2011)
**arXiv:** 1003.1296
**Relevance:** LOW (vortex foundations, not directly used in KK program)

---

## Abstract

We derive general expressions for the Kahler form of the L2-metric in terms of standard 2-forms on vortex moduli spaces. In the case of abelian vortices in gauged linear sigma-models, this allows us to compute explicitly the Kahler class of the L2-metric. As an application we compute the total volume of the moduli space of abelian semi-local vortices. In the strong coupling limit, this then leads to conjectural formulae for the volume of the space of holomorphic maps from a compact Riemann surface to projective space. Finally we show that the localization results of Samols in the abelian Higgs model extend to more general models. These include linear non-abelian vortices and vortices in gauged toric sigma-models.

---

## Key Results

1. **Kahler form of L2-metric**: A general formula is derived for the Kahler form omega_M of the L2-metric on the vortex moduli space M in terms of the curvature F_A of the universal connection and the pull-back of the equivariant Kahler form omega^G_X of the target.

2. **Simplification for linear targets**: When the target X is a complex vector space, the Kahler form omega_M is expressed solely in terms of the curvature F_A of the universal connection, generalizing a formula of Perutz for the abelian Higgs model.

3. **Explicit Kahler class for abelian models**: For abelian linear sigma-models (torus G acting with weights Q^a_j on X = C^n), the Kahler class [omega_M] is computed explicitly, generalizing the Manton-Nasir result for the abelian Higgs model.

4. **Volume of semi-local vortex moduli space**: The total volume and Einstein-Hilbert action of the moduli space of abelian semi-local vortices (G = U(1) on X = C^n) are computed.

5. **Conjectural formulae for holomorphic map spaces**: In the strong coupling limit e^2 -> infinity, conjectural formulae are obtained for the total volume of the space of holomorphic maps from a compact Riemann surface Sigma to CP^{n-1} equipped with the natural L2-metric.

6. **Samols localization**: The localization phenomenon discovered by Samols for the abelian Higgs model (the L2-metric localizes to contributions near vortex centers) extends to linear non-abelian vortices and vortices in gauged toric sigma-models, whenever the complexified G^C-action is free and transitive on a dense open subset of X.

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

This paper develops the technology for computing the natural L2-metric on vortex moduli spaces, including its Kahler class and total volume. The extension of Samols' localization beyond the abelian Higgs model to toric sigma-models is mathematically significant. The conjectural volume formulae for spaces of holomorphic maps Sigma -> CP^{n-1} connect vortex physics to enumerative geometry. These moduli space metrics and their volumes are part of Baptista's broader program understanding the geometry of gauge-theoretic solution spaces on compact manifolds.
