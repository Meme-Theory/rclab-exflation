## Abstract

We analyze the running at one-loop of the gauge couplings in the spectral Pati-Salam model that was derived in the framework of noncommutative geometry. There are a few different scenario's for the scalar particle content which are determined by the precise form of the Dirac operator for the finite noncommutative space. We consider these different scenarios and establish for all of them unification of the Pati-Salam gauge couplings. The boundary conditions are set by the usual RG flow for the Standard Model couplings at an intermediate mass scale at which the Pati-Salam symmetry is broken.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### Section I: Introduction

Two recent developments motivate this work: (1) inner fluctuations can be obtained without the order-one condition on D, and (2) the algebra M_2(H) + M_4(C) appears uniquely from the higher analogue of the Heisenberg commutation relations in NCG. This leads directly to the Pati-Salam gauge group SU(2)_R x SU(2)_L x SU(4), with gauge fields and scalar sector derived as inner perturbations of D.

The spectral action tr(f(D_A/Lambda)) expands as F_4 Lambda^4 a_0 + F_2 Lambda^2 a_2 + F_0 a_4 + ..., and requires gauge coupling unification g_R(Lambda) = g_L(Lambda) = g(Lambda).

### Section II: Spectral Pati-Salam and Grand Unification

The finite algebra is A_F = H_R + H_L + M_4(C). The Pati-Salam gauge group SU(2)_R x SU(2)_L x SU(4) is the inner automorphism group. The fermion content per family is 16 Weyl fermions: psi_{alpha I} with alpha = (dotted a, a) for SU(2)_R x SU(2)_L and I for SU(4). Explicitly:

psi = (nu_R, u_R^i, nu_L, u_L^i; e_R, d_R^i, e_L, d_L^i) with i = 1,2,3 color.

The finite Dirac operator D_F has specific zero components forced by chirality and real structure. Inner fluctuations produce gauge fields plus scalar Higgs fields in representations:
- Sigma^{bJ}_{aI}: (2_R, 2_L, 1) + (2_R, 2_L, 15)
- H_{aIbJ}: (1_R, 1_L, 6) + (1_R, 3_L, 10)
- H^{aI}_{bJ}: (1_R, 1_L, 6) + (3_R, 1_L, 10)

Three models are distinguished based on assumptions on D_F:

**Model A (Composite):** First-order condition satisfied. Fundamental scalars are phi^b_a (2,2,1), Sigma^J_I (1,1,15), Delta_{aJ} (2,1,4). The composite Higgs fields depend on Yukawa couplings.
- beta-functions: (b_R, b_L, b) = (7/3, 3, 31/3)
- Unification: Lambda ~ 2.5 x 10^15 GeV at m_R = 4.25 x 10^13 GeV

**Model B (Fundamental, no left-right symmetry):** H_{aIbJ} = 0. Fundamental Higgs in (2,2,1+15), (3_R,1,10), (1,1,6).
- beta-functions: (b_R, b_L, b) = (-26/3, -2, 2)
- Unification: Lambda ~ 6.3 x 10^16 GeV at m_R = 1.5 x 10^11 GeV

**Model C (Left-right symmetric):** Most general D_F. Full scalar content.
- beta-functions: (b_R, b_L, b) = (-26/3, -26/3, -4/3)
- Unification: Lambda ~ 2.7 x 10^15 GeV at m_R = 5.1 x 10^13 GeV

Boundary conditions at m_R (the Pati-Salam breaking scale):
1/g_1^2 = (2/3)/g^2 + 1/g_R^2, 1/g_2^2 = 1/g_L^2, 1/g_3^2 = 1/g^2.

### Section III: Conclusions

The spectral action requires (Eq. 37): F_0/(2 pi^2) g_L^2 = F_0/(2 pi^2) g_R^2 = F_0/(2 pi^2) g^2 = 1/4, demanding g_R = g_L = g. All three models achieve this unification, confirming validity of the spectral action at the corresponding scale. The unification scale is the scale at which the spectral action is valid as an effective theory.

## Key Results

1. All three variants of the spectral Pati-Salam model achieve gauge coupling unification at one-loop
2. The scalar particle content is uniquely determined by the Dirac operator — cannot be freely chosen
3. Model A (composite): Lambda ~ 2.5 x 10^15 GeV, m_R ~ 4 x 10^13 GeV
4. Model B (fundamental): Lambda ~ 6.3 x 10^16 GeV, m_R ~ 1.5 x 10^11 GeV
5. Model C (LR symmetric): Lambda ~ 2.7 x 10^15 GeV, m_R ~ 5 x 10^13 GeV
6. The 16 Weyl fermions per family is a general prediction of the spectral construction
7. The spectral action normalization demands g_R = g_L = g, which is achieved at the GUT scale
