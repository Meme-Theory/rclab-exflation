## Abstract

We consider the self-dual vortex equations on a positive line bundle L -> M over a compact Kahler manifold of arbitrary dimension. When M is simply connected, the moduli space M of vortex solutions is a projective space. When M is an abelian variety, M is the projectivization of the Fourier-Mukai transform L-hat -> M-hat over the dual variety. We extend this description of the moduli space to the abelian GLSM, i.e. to vortex equations with a torus gauge group acting linearly on a complex vector space. After establishing the Hitchin-Kobayashi correspondence appropriate for the general abelian GLSM, we give explicit descriptions of the moduli space M in the case where the manifold M is simply connected or is an abelian variety. In these examples we compute the Kahler class of the natural L2-metric on the moduli space. In the simplest cases we compute the volume and total scalar curvature of M. Finally, we note that for abelian GLSM the moduli space M is a compactification of the space of holomorphic maps from M to toric targets, just as in the usual case of M being a Riemann surface. This leads to various natural conjectures, for instance explicit formulae for the volume of the space of maps CP^m -> CP^n.

## Key Results

1. **Moduli space for simply connected M**: When M is a simply connected compact Kahler manifold, the vortex moduli space for a line bundle L is a projective space (the projectivization of the space of holomorphic sections of L).

2. **Moduli space for abelian varieties**: When M is an abelian variety, the moduli space is the projectivization of the Fourier-Mukai transform L-hat -> M-hat, a projective bundle over the dual variety.

3. **Hitchin-Kobayashi for abelian GLSM**: The Hitchin-Kobayashi correspondence is established for the general abelian gauged linear sigma model (torus gauge group acting on C^n) on higher-dimensional Kahler manifolds, interpreting tau-stability in this setting (Theorems 3.2, 3.3).

4. **Toric orbifold structure**: For simply connected M, the GLSM vortex moduli space is a toric orbifold. For abelian varieties, it is a toric fibration over a product of copies of the dual variety.

5. **L2-metric Kahler class**: The Kahler class of the natural L2-metric on the moduli space is computed for both simply connected and abelian variety cases, extending earlier results for Riemann surfaces.

6. **Compactification of holomorphic maps**: The GLSM moduli space is a compactification of the space of holomorphic maps from M to toric targets, generalizing the well-known correspondence for Riemann surfaces.

7. **Conjectural volume formulae**: The results lead to conjectures for the volume of the space of holomorphic maps CP^m -> CP^n equipped with the natural L2-metric.

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

This paper provides the most complete treatment of abelian vortex moduli spaces on higher-dimensional Kahler manifolds, going well beyond the Riemann surface case. The explicit description of moduli spaces as projective spaces (simply connected case) and Fourier-Mukai projectivizations (abelian variety case) gives concrete geometric models. The extension to the GLSM with toric targets and the compactification of holomorphic map spaces are directly relevant mathematical infrastructure for understanding field configurations on the compact internal spaces that appear in Kaluza-Klein reductions. The higher-dimensional Hitchin-Kobayashi correspondence is a key tool in Baptista's later KK work.
