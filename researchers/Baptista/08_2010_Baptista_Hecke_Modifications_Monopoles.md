# Non-abelian vortices, Hecke modifications and singular monopoles

**Author(s):** J. M. Baptista
**Year:** 2009 (published 2010)
**Journal:** Lett. Math. Phys. 92, 243 (2010)
**arXiv:** 0907.1752
**Relevance:** LOW (vortex foundations, not directly used in KK program)

---

## Abstract

In this note we show that for the group G = U(N) the space of Hecke modifications of a rank N vector bundle over a Riemann surface C coincides with the moduli space of solutions of certain non-abelian vortex equations over C. Through the recent work of Kapustin and Witten this then leads to an isomorphism between the moduli space of vortices and the moduli space of singular monopoles on the product of C with a closed interval I.

---

## Key Results

1. **Vortex-Hecke correspondence**: The space of non-abelian vortex internal structures I_N (characterized by integers k_0 and sequences of subspaces V_j of C^N) coincides with the space of Hecke modifications of a rank N vector bundle over a Riemann surface C for the group G = U(N).

2. **Dictionary between types**: Hecke modifications of type (m_1, ..., m_N) with m_1 >= ... >= m_N >= 0 correspond to vortex internal structures with k_0 = m_N and dim V_k = #{j : m_j - m_N - k >= 0}.

3. **Vortex-monopole isomorphism**: Through Kapustin-Witten's work relating Hecke modifications to singular Bogomolny monopoles on I x C, the vortex moduli space is isomorphic to the monopole moduli space (Proposition 4.2). The monopole connection is related by a complex gauge transformation on the boundary C+ to the vortex connection.

4. **Affine Grassmannian description**: The space of all Hecke modifications of a rank N vector bundle at a point coincides with the affine Grassmannian Gr_N for GL(N,C). The vortex internal structures give a concrete finite-dimensional parametrization of this space.

5. **Compactification via surjective map**: The natural compactification of spaces of Hecke modifications of fixed type described by Kapustin-Witten corresponds to the surjective map phi: Gr(k_1,N) x ... x Gr(k_l,N) -> Y(m_1,...,m_N), analogous to the vortex internal structure compactification.

6. **Open questions**: (a) Whether vortices are literal boundaries of singular monopoles (boundary condition (13)); (b) Whether meromorphic vortex solutions can handle negative m_j's; (c) Relation between Hanany-Tong ADHM construction and Nahm equations with singularities.

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

This paper connects non-abelian vortex moduli spaces to Hecke modifications (a central object in the geometric Langlands program via Kapustin-Witten) and to singular monopoles on three-manifolds. The correspondence between 2D vortices and 3D monopoles via the Hitchin-Kobayashi mechanism is an example of dimensional uplift/reduction relating gauge-theoretic objects in different dimensions -- the same structural theme that appears in Baptista's Kaluza-Klein program relating higher-dimensional gauge fields to lower-dimensional sigma-model configurations.
