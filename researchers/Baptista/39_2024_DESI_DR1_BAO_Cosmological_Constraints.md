## Abstract

We present cosmological results from the measurement of baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO) in galaxy, quasar and Lyman-$\alpha$ forest tracers from the first year of observations from the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI), to be released in the DESI Data Release 1. DESI BAO provide robust measurements of the transverse comoving distance and Hubble rate, or their combination, relative to the sound horizon, in seven redshift bins from over 6 million extragalactic objects in the redshift range $0.1 < z < 4.2$. To mitigate confirmation bias, a blind analysis was implemented to measure the BAO scales. DESI BAO data alone are consistent with the standard flat $\Lambda$CDM cosmological model with a matter density $\Omega_m = 0.295 \pm 0.015$. Paired with a baryon density prior from Big Bang Nucleosynthesis and the robustly measured acoustic angular scale from the cosmic microwave background (CMB), DESI requires $H_0 = (68.52 \pm 0.62)$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$. In conjunction with CMB anisotropies from Planck and CMB lensing data from Planck and ACT, we find $\Omega_m = 0.307 \pm 0.005$ and $H_0 = (67.97 \pm 0.38)$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$. Extending the baseline model with a constant dark energy equation of state parameter $w$, DESI BAO alone require $w = -0.99^{+0.15}_{-0.13}$. In models with a time-varying dark energy equation of state parametrised by $w_0$ and $w_a$, combinations of DESI with CMB or with type Ia supernovae (SN Ia) individually prefer $w_0 > -1$ and $w_a < 0$. This preference is 2.6$\sigma$ for the DESI+CMB combination, and persists or grows when SN Ia are added in, giving results discrepant with the $\Lambda$CDM model at the 2.5$\sigma$, 3.5$\sigma$ or 3.9$\sigma$ levels for the addition of the Pantheon+, Union3, or DES-SN5YR supernova datasets respectively. For the flat $\Lambda$CDM model with the sum of neutrino mass $\sum m_\nu$ free, combining the DESI and CMB data yields an upper limit $\sum m_\nu < 0.072$ (0.113) eV at 95% confidence for a $\sum m_\nu > 0$ ($\sum m_\nu > 0.059$) eV prior.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### Section 2: Data and Methodology

DESI DR1 covers 7,500 deg$^2$ (about half of the final 14,200 deg$^2$) in the redshift range $0.1 < z < 4.2$. Six tracer classes: BGS ($0.1 < z < 0.4$, 300,017 redshifts), LRG ($0.4 < z < 1.1$, 2,138,600 redshifts), ELG ($0.8 < z < 1.6$, 2,432,022 redshifts), QSO ($0.8 < z < 2.1$, 856,652 redshifts), and Ly$\alpha$ forest ($1.77 < z < 4.16$, 709,565 quasars). Total: over 6 million extragalactic objects.

BAO measurements constrain $D_M(z)/r_d$ and $D_H(z)/r_d$ (or $D_V(z)/r_d$ for low-SNR bins), where $D_M$ is transverse comoving distance, $D_H = c/H(z)$, and $r_d$ is the sound horizon at the drag epoch.

### Section 4: LCDM Constraints

DESI BAO alone: $\Omega_m = 0.295 \pm 0.015$, $r_dh = (101.8 \pm 1.3)$ Mpc.

DESI+CMB: $\Omega_m = 0.3069 \pm 0.0050$, $H_0 = (67.97 \pm 0.38)$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$.

### Section 5.1: Flat wCDM Model

DESI BAO alone: $w = -0.99^{+0.15}_{-0.13}$ (consistent with $\Lambda$CDM).

DESI+CMB: $w = -1.122^{+0.062}_{-0.054}$ (mild preference for phantom DE).

DESI+CMB+Pantheon+: $w = -0.997 \pm 0.025$.

### Section 5.2: Flat $w_0 w_a$CDM Model

This is the key result. Using the CPL parametrization $w(a) = w_0 + w_a(1-a)$:

**DESI BAO alone:** $w_0 = -0.55^{+0.39}_{-0.21}$, $w_a < -1.32$ (poorly constrained, but prefers $w_0 > -1$ and $w_a < 0$).

**DESI+CMB:** $w_0 = -0.45^{+0.34}_{-0.21}$, $w_a = -1.79^{+0.48}_{-1.0}$ (2.6$\sigma$ tension with $\Lambda$CDM).

**DESI+CMB+Pantheon+:** $w_0 = -0.827 \pm 0.063$, $w_a = -0.75^{+0.29}_{-0.25}$ (2.5$\sigma$).

**DESI+CMB+Union3:** $w_0 = -0.65 \pm 0.10$, $w_a = -1.27^{+0.40}_{-0.34}$ (3.5$\sigma$).

**DESI+CMB+DESY5:** $w_0 = -0.727 \pm 0.067$, $w_a = -1.05^{+0.31}_{-0.27}$ (3.9$\sigma$).

All combinations prefer $w_0 > -1$ (quintessence-like today) and $w_a < 0$ (DE was more phantom-like in the past), consistent with a "Quintom B" crossing scenario.

### Section 7: Neutrino Constraints

DESI+CMB: $\sum m_\nu < 0.072$ eV (95%, physical prior $\sum m_\nu > 0$) or $\sum m_\nu < 0.113$ eV (95%, prior $\sum m_\nu > 0.059$ eV). These are substantially relaxed in non-$\Lambda$CDM backgrounds.

## Key Results

1. DESI BAO alone gives $\Omega_m = 0.295 \pm 0.015$ in flat $\Lambda$CDM -- consistent with Planck.
2. Combined DESI+BBN+$\theta_*$: $H_0 = (68.52 \pm 0.62)$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$.
3. In constant-$w$ models, DESI BAO alone gives $w = -0.99^{+0.15}_{-0.13}$ -- consistent with cosmological constant.
4. In the $w_0 w_a$CDM model, all DESI combinations with CMB and/or SN Ia prefer $w_0 > -1$ and $w_a < 0$, with significance ranging from 2.5$\sigma$ to 3.9$\sigma$ depending on SN dataset.
5. DESI+CMB gives the tightest neutrino mass bound: $\sum m_\nu < 0.072$ eV (95% CL).
6. Spatial curvature is consistent with zero: $\Omega_K = 0.0024 \pm 0.0016$ (DESI+CMB).

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

DESI DR1 is a Level 1 observational test for the phonon-exflation framework. The preference for $w_0 > -1$ and $w_a < 0$ (dynamical dark energy crossing $w = -1$) at 2.5--3.9$\sigma$ is precisely the signature the framework predicts if dark energy arises from spectral properties of the internal manifold that evolve with tau. The "Quintom B" crossing pattern ($w < -1$ in the past, $w > -1$ today) aligns with the framework's prediction that the equation of state should evolve as the internal geometry transits through the fold. However, the framework's current mechanism (instanton gas) must quantitatively reproduce $w(z)$ matching these constraints. The DESI-measured $\Omega_m = 0.295 \pm 0.015$ and $H_0 = 68.52 \pm 0.62$ km/s/Mpc are the benchmarks any framework prediction must match. The neutrino mass bound $\sum m_\nu < 0.072$ eV constrains the normal hierarchy and may be relevant to the framework's PMNS predictions.
