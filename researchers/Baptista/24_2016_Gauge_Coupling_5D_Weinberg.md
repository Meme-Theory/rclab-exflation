# The Evolution of Gauge Couplings and the Weinberg Angle in 5 Dimensions for an SU(3) Gauge Group

**Author(s):** Mohammed Omer Khojali, A. S. Cornell and Aldo Deandrea
**Year:** 2016
**Journal:** (Conference proceedings)
**arXiv:** 1602.07441
**Relevance:** HIGH

---

## Abstract

We test in a simplified 5-dimensional model with SU(3) gauge symmetry, the evolution equations of the gauge couplings of a model containing bulk fields, gauge fields and one pair of fermions. In this model we assume that the fermion doublet and two singlet fields are located at fixed points of the extra-dimension compactified on an S1/Z2 orbifold. The gauge coupling evolution is derived at one-loop in 5-dimensions, for the gauge group G = SU(3), and used to test the impact on lower energy observables, in particular the Weinberg angle. The gauge bosons and the Higgs field arise from the gauge bosons in 5 dimensions, as in a gauge-Higgs model. The model is used as a testing ground as it is not a complete and realistic model for the electroweak interactions.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### Section 1: Introduction

The paper studies a 5D gauge theory with gauge group G = SU(3) compactified on S1/Z2 orbifold. Interactions at low energies may be unified, and distinct 4D fields can be integrated as a single multiplet in higher dimensions. The Higgs field arises as a component of 5D gauge fields (gauge-Higgs unification).

The orbifold projection P = e^{i pi lambda_3} = diag(-1, -1, 1) breaks SU(3) -> SU(2) x U(1) = H. The coset G/H has dimension 8 - 4 = 4, giving 4 Goldstone bosons. The massless 4D fields include gauge bosons A^a_mu in the adjoint of H and a charged scalar doublet from A^a_5.

### Fermion Sector

Brane fields: a left-handed doublet Q_L = (u_L, d_L) and two right-handed singlets u_R, d_R, localized at fixed points y_1 and y_2 (either 0 or pi R). Bulk fermion psi_a has KK mode expansion:

psi_a(y) = (1/sqrt(2 pi R)) psi^0_{aR}(x) + (1/sqrt(2 pi R)) sum_{n=1}^infty [sin(ny/R) psi^n_{aL}(x) + cos(ny/R) psi^n_{aR}(x)]

The 4D Lagrangian is obtained by integrating out the y coordinate, yielding standard gauge interactions plus KK tower contributions.

### Section 2: Gauge Coupling Evolution

The beta functions in 5D are:
16 pi^2 dg_i/dt = b^SM_i g_i^3 + (b_i + S(t) b-tilde_i) g_i^3

where t = ln(S(t)/M_Z R), S(t) = mu R for M_Z < mu < 1/(M_Z R).

The numerical coefficients are:
- b^SM = (41/10, -19/6, -7)
- b = (10/3, -51/16, -20/3, 3/8)
- b-tilde = (45/(16 pi), -15/(16 pi), -5/pi, 0)

### Section 3: Results

The gauge couplings g_1, g_2, g_3 are run for compactification scales R^{-1} = 1, 2, 10 TeV. Approximate unification occurs at t ~ 7 for R^{-1} = 1 TeV.

The Weinberg angle from SU(3) group theory arguments is sin^2(theta_W) = 0.75. The model achieves sin^2(theta_W) ~ 0.69 at compactification scale R^{-1} = 1 TeV, closer to the group-theoretic prediction than the SM value of ~0.23 at low energies.

## Key Results

1. SU(3) gauge-Higgs unification in 5D on S1/Z2 achieves approximate gauge coupling unification
2. The orbifold projection P = diag(-1,-1,1) breaks SU(3) -> SU(2) x U(1)
3. The Higgs doublet arises from A^a_5 components of the 5D gauge field
4. The Weinberg angle evolves toward the SU(3) prediction sin^2(theta_W) = 3/4 at high energies
5. For R^{-1} = 1 TeV, sin^2(theta_W) ~ 0.69 at the unification scale
