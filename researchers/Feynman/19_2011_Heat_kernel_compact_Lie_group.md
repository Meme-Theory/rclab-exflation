# The Asymptotic Expansion of the Heat Kernel on a Compact Lie Group

**Author(s):** Seunghun Hong
**Year:** 2011
**Journal:** preprint (Pennsylvania State University)
**arXiv/DOI:** arXiv:1111.2643v1 [math.FA] (11 Nov 2011)
**Relevance:** HIGH

---

## Abstract

Let $G$ be a compact connected Lie group equipped with a bi-invariant metric. We calculate the asymptotic expansion of the heat kernel of the laplacian on $G$ and the heat trace using Lie algebra methods. The Duflo isomorphism plays a key role.

---

## Key Arguments and Derivations

**Section 1 (Introduction).** The Laplacian $\Delta$ on a compact Riemannian manifold $M$ is determined by, and determines, the metric. Weyl's law bounds the eigenvalue counting function $N(\lambda)/\lambda^{n/2} = \operatorname{vol}(\Omega)/[(4\pi)^{n/2}\Gamma(n/2+1)] + O(1/\lambda)$; the heat-trace partition function $Z(t) = \sum_k e^{-t\lambda_k}$ has the Minakshisundaram–Pleijel expansion $Z(t)\sim (4\pi t)^{-\dim M/2}(a_0 + a_1 t + a_2 t^2 + \cdots)$ with $a_0 = \operatorname{vol}(M)$ and $a_1 = \tfrac16\int_M S$ (McKean–Singer). The paper computes this expansion when $M = G$ is a compact connected Lie group with a bi-invariant metric.

**Section 2 (Preliminaries).**
- *Laplacian.* $\Delta_G f = \operatorname{div}(\operatorname{grad} f) = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{\det g}} \partial_i(\sqrt{\det g}\, g^{ij}\partial_j f)$. Bi-invariance of the metric makes $\Delta_G$ bi-invariant. In an orthonormal basis $\{X_i\}$ of $\mathfrak{g}$, $\Delta_G = \sum_i \tilde X_i \tilde X_i$ (left-invariant fields generated by the basis); the corresponding element of the universal enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak{g})$ is the Casimir $\operatorname{Cas} = \sum_i X_i X_i \in Z(\mathfrak{g})$.
- *Spectrum of $\Delta$.* $(1-\Delta_G)^{-1}$ is compact on $L^2(G)$; the eigenfunctions $\{u_k\}$ form an orthonormal basis and eigenvalues $0 \ge -\lambda_1 \ge -\lambda_2 \ge\cdots$ (nonincreasing).
- *Heat kernel.* $e^{t\Delta_G}$ is an integral operator with smooth kernel $K_t(x,y) = k_t(x^{-1}y)$; convolution kernel $k_t$. Expansion $k_t \sim h_t(a_0 + a_1 t + a_2 t^2 + \cdots)$ as $t\to 0^+$ with Gaussian $h_t(X) = e^{-\|X\|^2/4t}/(4\pi t)^{\dim M/2}$ under the exponential chart near $e\in G$. The asymptotic series $s_t = h_t \sum a_i t^i$ satisfies $(\partial_t + \Delta_G) s_t = 0$.
- *Algebra.* $U(\mathfrak{g})$ is the universal enveloping algebra; $Z(\mathfrak{g}) = U(\mathfrak{g})^{\mathfrak{g}}$ is the centre, isomorphic (as associative algebras) to the algebra of bi-invariant differential operators on $G$. Bi-invariance of the metric forces the Lie-algebraic and Riemannian exponential maps to coincide, so the exponential chart is geodesic and $g_{ij}(e) = \delta_{ij}$ with $\partial_k g_{ij}(e) = 0$, hence $\Delta_G$ at the identity is $\sum \partial^2/\partial x_i^2$.
- *Duflo isomorphism.* $\operatorname{Duf}: S(\mathfrak{g})^\mathfrak{g} \to Z(\mathfrak{g})$, realised as $j\cdot\exp_*$ where $j(X) = \det^{1/2}\!\big(\tfrac{\sinh(\operatorname{ad}_X/2)}{\operatorname{ad}_X/2}\big)$.

**Section 3 (Main computation).**
*Step 1 (image of the flat Laplacian under Duf).* Let $\Delta_\mathfrak{g} = \sum X_i X_i \in S(\mathfrak{g})^\mathfrak{g}$ be the flat Laplacian on the Euclidean space $\mathfrak{g}$. Then
$$\operatorname{Duf}(\Delta_\mathfrak{g}) = \operatorname{Cas} + \tfrac{1}{24}\operatorname{tr}_\mathfrak{g}(\operatorname{Cas}).$$
By Kostant's identity $\tfrac{1}{24}\operatorname{tr}_\mathfrak{g}\operatorname{Cas} = -\langle\rho,\rho\rangle$ (half-sum of positive roots), so $\operatorname{Duf}(\Delta_\mathfrak{g}) = \Delta_G - \langle\rho,\rho\rangle$.

*Step 2 (Lemma 3.5).* When $\operatorname{Duf}(\Delta_\mathfrak{g})$ is expressed on $G$ in the exponential chart, it satisfies $\operatorname{Duf}(\Delta_\mathfrak{g})^{\exp} = j^{-1}\circ\Delta_\mathfrak{g}\circ j$.

*Step 3 (Lemma 3.6).* The convolution kernel $p_t$ of $e^{t\operatorname{Duf}(\Delta_\mathfrak{g})}$ has asymptotic expansion $p_t^{\exp} = p_t\circ\exp \sim h_t \cdot j^{-1}$ near $0\in\mathfrak{g}$, because $h_t/j$ is the formal solution of $(\partial_t - j^{-1}\Delta_\mathfrak{g} j) s_t = 0$ and $h_t$ satisfies the flat heat equation $(\partial_t - \Delta_\mathfrak{g}) h_t = 0$.

*Step 4 (Lemma 3.8, scalar curvature).* For a compact connected Lie group with bi-invariant metric, the Riemann tensor reduces to $\operatorname{Rm}(\tilde X,\tilde Y,\tilde Z,\tilde W) = -\tfrac14\langle [X,Y],[Z,W]\rangle$ (using $\nabla_{\tilde X}\tilde Y = \tfrac12[\tilde X,\tilde Y]$). Summing over an orthonormal basis gives the scalar curvature $S = -\tfrac14 \operatorname{tr}_\mathfrak{g}(\operatorname{Cas})$.

*Step 5 (Theorem 3.9).* Combining: $e^{t\Delta_G} = e^{tS/6} e^{t\operatorname{Duf}(\Delta_\mathfrak{g})}$, hence
$$k_t^{\exp}(X) = k_t(\exp X) \sim \frac{h_t(X)}{j(X)} e^{tS/6}$$
as $t\to 0^+$.

*Step 6 (Corollary 3.10, heat-trace).* The partition function has exact (in the asymptotic sense) form
$$Z(t) = \operatorname{tr}(e^{t\Delta_G}) \sim \operatorname{vol}(G) e^{tS/6}$$
as $t\to 0^+$.

## Key Results

1. For a compact connected Lie group with a bi-invariant metric, the full asymptotic expansion of the heat kernel is captured by a single non-perturbative factor $j^{-1}e^{tS/6}$ times the Gaussian.
2. The image of the flat Laplacian on $\mathfrak{g}$ under the Duflo isomorphism is the Casimir plus a numerical shift $-\langle\rho,\rho\rangle$.
3. Scalar curvature is $S = -\tfrac14\operatorname{tr}_\mathfrak{g}(\operatorname{Cas})$.
4. Heat trace: $Z(t) \sim \operatorname{vol}(G)\, e^{tS/6}$ (all Minakshisundaram–Pleijel coefficients expressed in closed form on bi-invariant Lie groups).

## Relevance to Phonon-Exflation

Provides a closed-form heat kernel on compact Lie groups with bi-invariant metrics — directly applicable to the Jensen-deformed SU(3) fibre when the deformation is interpreted as a small perturbation of a bi-invariant base. The Duflo isomorphism reduces heat-trace calculations on the fibre to Euclidean Gaussians modulated by the Jacobian $j(X)$, short-circuiting the Seeley–DeWitt series for the unperturbed piece. The scalar-curvature identity $S = -\tfrac14 \operatorname{tr}_\mathfrak{g}\operatorname{Cas}$ is the technical tool used in computation computations of the $a_2$ spectral moment on SU(3), underpinning the Einstein–Hilbert identification (two-layer gravity, S50–S51 atlas). The $e^{tS/6}$ factor matches the $R$-summed Schwinger–DeWitt resummation quoted in Vassilevich's "user's manual" (paper 15) and used in Parker/Wondrak-type gravitational-pair-creation arguments (paper 23).
