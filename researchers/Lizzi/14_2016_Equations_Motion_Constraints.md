# Equations of Motion as Constraints: Superselection Rules, Ward Identities

**Authors:** M. Asorey, A.P. Balachandran, Fedele Lizzi, G. Marmo
**Year:** 2016
**arXiv:** 1612.05886v2

---

## Abstract

We study the role of equations of motion as constraints in gauge theories and gravity. Using local quantum physics framework, we show how equations of motion generate asymptotic transformation groups, creating superselection sectors that differentiate infrared physics. Ward identities emerge as natural consequences of gauge invariance in this formulation.

---

## Key Results

1. **Equations as constraints**: EOM are not just dynamical relations but impose **topological constraints** on the observable algebra.

2. **Superselection from IR**: Different IR regimes (different asymptotic behavior at infinity) lead to inequivalent quantum sectors separated by superselection rules.

3. **BMS group**: Bondi-Metzner-Sachs asymptotic symmetry group emerges naturally, explaining why it produces superselection sectors (charge conservation at infinity).

4. **Ward identities**: Gauge invariance enforces specific relationships between amplitudes (Ward identities), derivable from equation-of-motion constraints.

## Connection to Phonon-Exflation

**Framework application**: In GGE relic formation (post-transit), equations of motion for collective excitations become constraints that **define the allowed particle creation channels**. This may explain why only specific quasiparticle types form (Leggett channels) with specific statistics.

The framework's claim that GGE relic is "ordered" (not thermal) might be a **consequence of superselection structure** imposed by equations of motion in the post-transit phase.
