# Localizability in κ-Minkowski Spacetime

**Authors:** Fedele Lizzi, M. Manfredonia, F. Mercati
**Year:** 2019
**arXiv:** 1912.07098v1

---

## Abstract

Using quantum mechanics methods, we study κ-Minkowski space as a quantum space with noncommuting self-adjoint coordinate operators. Mellin transform replaces Fourier transforms. Complete sets of commuting observables cannot simultaneously include all coordinates—fundamental limitation on localization.

---

## Key Results

1. **κ-deformed uncertainty**: Δt · Δφ ≥ κ (time-angle deformation)

2. **Mellin localization**: Position basis obtained via Mellin transform, not Fourier

3. **Localization hierarchy**: Can localize in space OR time, not both—choice depends on observer frame

4. **Transformation properties**: Observer-dependent localization related to κ-Poincaré group coaction

---

## Connection to Phonon-Exflation

**Application**: The framework's phonons cannot be arbitrarily well-localized. A phonon has intrinsic size ~ κ (noncommutativity scale). This provides a **fundamental mechanism for phonon delocalization** and explains why they don't behave as point particles—they are extended objects in the fabric.
