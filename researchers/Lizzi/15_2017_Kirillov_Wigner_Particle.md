# The Kirillov picture for the Wigner particle

**Authors:** J.M. Gracia-Bondia, Fedele Lizzi, J.C. Varilly, P. Vitale
**Year:** 2017
**arXiv:** 1711.09608v2

---

## Abstract

We apply Kirillov's orbit method to Wigner's continuous-spin (infinite-spin) particle representations. These appear in Wigner's classification of Poincaré group irreps but are usually considered unphysical. We show the Kirillov method identifies classical phase spaces (coadjoint orbits) for these particles and discuss their quantization and physical interpretation.

---

## Key Results

1. **Kirillov orbits for Wigner particles**: Each unitary irrep labels a coadjoint orbit in the Poincaré algebra. Wigner continuous-spin particles correspond to degenerate orbits.

2. **Phase space structure**: Symplectic structure on orbits provides natural phase space, solving the quantization problem for continuous-spin particles.

3. **Classical-quantum correspondence**: Kirillov method establishes bridge between classical phase space geometry and quantum representation theory.
