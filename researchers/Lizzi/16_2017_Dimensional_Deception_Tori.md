# Dimensional Deception from Noncommutative Tori: An alternative to Horava-Lifschitz

**Authors:** Fedele Lizzi, Aleksandr Pinzul
**Year:** 2017
**arXiv:** 1711.01461v1

---

## Abstract

Studying fuzzy (quantized) tori with deformed Laplacian operators, we show that scaling dimension flows from 2 in the infrared to 1 in the ultraviolet. This dimension flow is achieved by rearrangement of degrees of freedom, not by external anisotropy. The result provides an alternative to Horava-Lifschitz models of quantum gravity without requiring preferred spatial directions.

---

## Key Results

1. **Running dimension**: The effective dimension probed by particles changes with energy scale—IR: dim=2, UV: dim=1.

2. **No preferred direction**: Unlike Horava-Lifschitz, the anisotropy emerges dynamically, preserving isotropy at each scale.

3. **Fuzzy geometry**: Noncommutative geometry naturally provides the quantum deformation needed for dimension flow.

4. **Renormalization group interpretation**: Dimension flow is equivalent to RG running with anomalous dimensions.
