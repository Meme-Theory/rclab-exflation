# The Momentum Spaces of κ-Minkowski noncommutative spacetime

**Authors:** Fedele Lizzi, F. Mercati, M. Manfredonia
**Year:** 2020
**arXiv:** 2001.08756v3

---

## Abstract

κ-Minkowski spacetime admits multiple inequivalent momentum space geometries. We unify these approaches, relating them to different signatures and symmetry group choices. Three momentum spaces are identified as half de Sitter, anti-de Sitter, and Minkowski spaces, each associated with a κ-Poincaré group deformation.

---

## Key Results

1. **Curved momentum spaces**: κ-deformation maps flat momentum space to curved (dS, AdS, or Minkowski) geometry.

2. **Three inequivalent structures**: Different quantization orderings or signature choices yield different momentum space metrics.

3. **Unified framework**: Both approaches (invariant metrics on Lie groups and 5D matrix representations) yield consistent geometry.

4. **Carroll group connection**: New momentum space identified as light cone, associated with κ-deformation of Carroll group (non-relativistic limit).
