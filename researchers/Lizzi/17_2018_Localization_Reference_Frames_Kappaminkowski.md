# Localization and Reference Frames in κ-Minkowski Spacetime

**Authors:** Fedele Lizzi, M. Manfredonia, F. Mercati, T. Poulain
**Year:** 2018
**arXiv:** 1811.08409v1

---

## Abstract

We study limitations to localizability of events and reference frames in κ-Minkowski noncommutative spacetime. Spacetime coordinates are treated as noncommuting quantum operators. A complete set of commuting observables cannot include both radial and time coordinates simultaneously—the transformation between different sets is the Mellin transform.

---

## Key Results

1. **Deformed uncertainty relations**: Δx · Δp ≥ κℏ (κ = noncommutativity parameter).

2. **Mellin transform**: Switch between spatial and temporal localization bases via Mellin transform, not Fourier.

3. **Observer limits**: Different observers cannot simultaneously localize events with arbitrary precision—fundamental quantum gravity effect.

4. **κ-Poincaré symmetry**: Quantum group symmetry structure preserves deformed uncertainty relations consistently.
