# Localization and observers in ρ-Minkowski spacetime

**Authors:** Fedele Lizzi, L. Scala, P. Vitale
**Year:** 2022
**arXiv:** 2205.10862v2

---

## Abstract

We study quantum observers and localizability in ρ-Minkowski spacetime (angular noncommutativity). Quantum observers are described by states in the Hilbert space with limited localization properties. We analyze how reference frame transformations affect observer localizability under the ρ-Poincaré quantum group.

---

## Key Results

1. **ρ-observers**: States with localization uncertainty Δt·Δφ ~ ρ.

2. **Frame covariance**: Observer transformations under ρ-Poincaré coaction preserve uncertainty relations.

3. **Superobserver paradox avoided**: Multiple observers with different localization limits are consistent (no paradox due to fuzzy locality).

4. **Angular resolution**: Angular measurements have intrinsic limit ~ ρ; temporal localization complementary.
