# Ultra high energetic neutrinos in their rest frame: can their time of arrival be determined?

**Authors:** Fedele Lizzi
**Year:** 2025
**arXiv:** 2509.24516v3

---

## Abstract

Analysis of ultra-high-energy neutrinos (UHE, E ~ 10²⁰ eV) observed by KM3NeT collaboration. In the neutrino rest frame, after creation, a muon arrives and interacts. Due to wave packet spreading, time of interaction becomes uncertain. In lab frame, this uncertainty is boosted by Lorentz factor (~10¹⁸), becoming macroscopic. This prevents observation of bursts or correlation with other events.

---

## Key Results

### 1. Rest Frame Analysis
- UHE neutrino created with finite wave packet width
- Non-relativistic Gaussian wave packet: initial width Δx₀ ~ 10⁻²⁰ m
- Wave packet spreads: Δx(t) = Δx₀ √[1 + (ℏt/(2m Δx₀²))²]

### 2. Muon Interaction Uncertainty
When muon reaches neutrino, time of interaction has uncertainty:

$$\Delta t_\text{rest} \sim \frac{\hbar}{m_\nu c^2} \cdot \frac{1}{\Delta x_0} \sim 10^{-30} \text{ s}$$

Small in rest frame.

### 3. Boost to Lab Frame
Lorentz transformation magnifies time uncertainty:

$$\Delta t_\text{lab} = \gamma \Delta t_\text{rest} \sim 10^{18} \times 10^{-30} \text{ s} \sim 10^{-12} \text{ s}$$

**Macroscopic uncertainty** (~1 nanosecond to microsecond), comparable to burst durations.

### 4. Implications
- Cannot determine exact time of muon creation from neutrino interaction
- Bursts cannot be correlated with other events
- Timing information is lost in rest frame of UHE neutrino
- For lower-energy neutrinos (< 10¹⁹ eV), uncertainty is smaller

---

## Key Findings

1. **Wave packet interpretation essential**: UHE neutrinos are not point-like; wave packet spreading dominates at extreme energies.

2. **Lorentz boost amplifies uncertainty**: What is tiny in rest frame becomes macroscopic in lab frame.

3. **Observable consequence**: KM3NeT observations constrained by fundamental wave-packet physics, not just detector resolution.

4. **Energy threshold**: Below E ~ 10¹⁹ eV, uncertainty becomes manageable (predictable from quantum mechanics).

This suggests the framework might predict **observational limits on acoustic phonon correlations**—timestamps in acoustic experiments would show systematic uncertainty for high-energy quasiparticles.
