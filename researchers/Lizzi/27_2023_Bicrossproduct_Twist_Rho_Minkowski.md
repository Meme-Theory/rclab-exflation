# Bicrossproduct vs. twist quantum symmetries in noncommutative geometries: the case of ρ-Minkowski

**Authors:** G. Fabiano, G. Gubitosi, Fedele Lizzi, L. Scala, P. Vitale
**Year:** 2023
**arXiv:** 2305.00526v2

---

## Abstract

We analyze quantum Poincaré symmetries of ρ-Minkowski spacetime (angular noncommutativity) showing both bicrossproduct and Drinfel'd twist formulations are possible. We derive a new cyclic ⋆-product, unify the two approaches, and clarify their relationship.

---

## Key Results

1. **Bicrossproduct symmetry**: Quantum group structure with both left and right actions.

2. **Twist formulation**: Alternative covariance via Drinfel'd twist of commutative structure.

3. **New cyclic ⋆-product**: Star product with cyclic property under standard integral measure.

4. **Unified framework**: Both approaches give equivalent physics despite different mathematical structures.
