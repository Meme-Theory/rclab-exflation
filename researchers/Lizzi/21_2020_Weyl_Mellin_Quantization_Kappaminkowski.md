# The Weyl-Mellin quantization map for κ-Minkowski Noncommutative Spacetime

**Authors:** A. Carotenuto, Fedele Lizzi, M. Manfredonia, F. Mercati
**Year:** 2020
**arXiv:** 2011.10628v2

---

## Abstract

We present a quantization map (Weyl-Mellin quantization) for κ-Minkowski space that reproduces the correct commutation relations and maps square-integrable functions to Hilbert-Schmidt operators, with real functions mapping to self-adjoint operators. The map is based on Mellin transform on radial and temporal coordinates.

---

## Key Results

1. **Weyl-Mellin quantization**: Generalizes Weyl quantization to κ-deformed spaces

2. **Proper operator properties**: Square-integrable → Hilbert-Schmidt, real functions → self-adjoint

3. **Modified ⋆-product**: Deformed star product derived from Weyl-Mellin map with concrete examples

4. **Consistent with κ-symmetry**: Quantization respects κ-Poincaré group structure
