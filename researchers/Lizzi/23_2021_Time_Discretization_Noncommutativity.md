# Time Discretization From Noncommutativity

**Authors:** Fedele Lizzi, P. Vitale
**Year:** 2021
**arXiv:** 2101.06633v2

---

## Abstract

In ρ-Minkowski spacetime (angular noncommutativity: [t,φ]=iρ), time becomes discrete. The spectrum of time eigenvalues is equally spaced by the noncommutativity scale. Time intervals are quantized even though absolute time can be any real value.

---

## Key Arguments

### 1. ρ-Minkowski Algebra
Define angular noncommutativity:
$$[t, \phi] = i\rho$$
$$[x_i, x_j] = 0, \quad [x_i, p_j] = i\hbar\delta_{ij}$$

where ρ is the noncommutativity scale (not related to Planck constant).

### 2. Time Operator Spectrum
The time coordinate t acts as an operator on the Hilbert space. Its **spectrum is discrete**:

$$\text{spec}(t) = \{... -2\rho, -\rho, 0, \rho, 2\rho, ...\}$$

Equally spaced eigenvalues separated by ρ.

### 3. Time Uncertainty
- Absolute time value: can measure to arbitrary precision (continuous spectrum of possible outcomes)
- Time intervals: Δt = n·ρ (n = integer) — **quantized**
- Duration measurements: fundamentally discrete

### 4. Self-adjoint Extensions
Possible self-adjoint extensions of time operator yield:
- All eigenvalues real (physical time)
- Spacing invariant under extension choice
- But ambiguity in zero-point of time (phase freedom)

---

## Key Results

1. **Time is intrinsically discrete**: Below ρ-scale, temporal ordering becomes discrete.

2. **Duration quantization**: Δt must be integer multiple of ρ.

3. **Quantum gravity origin**: If ρ ~ ℓ_P (Planck length), this provides **fundamental mechanism for time quantization** without ad-hoc assumptions.

4. **Causal structure modified**: Discrete time intervals imply modified light-cone structure (time-displaced causality).
