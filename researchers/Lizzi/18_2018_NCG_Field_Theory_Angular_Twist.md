# Noncommutative field theory from angular twist

**Authors:** Marija Dimitrijevic Ciric, Nikola Konjik, M.A. Kurkov, Fedele Lizzi, P. Vitale
**Year:** 2018
**arXiv:** 1806.06678v1

---

## Abstract

We construct NCG field theory based on angular (ρ-Minkowski) noncommutativity. The ⋆-product is derived from a twisted Poincaré transformation. The field theory is invariant under twisted Poincaré symmetry, with modified dispersion relations and UV/IR mixing properties.

---

## Key Results

1. **Angular noncommutativity**: [t, φ] = iρ (time-azimuthal angle deformation)

2. **Twisted Poincaré invariance**: ⋆-product is covariant under deformed symmetry transformations

3. **Modified UV/IR behavior**: λφ⁴ one-loop shows unusual renormalization (UV/IR mixing present but modified)

4. **Particle decay**: Momentum non-commutativity leads to novel kinematic constraints on decay processes

---

## Connection to Phonon-Exflation

**Application**: If phonons obey angular noncommutativity (temporal acoustic ordering relations), this framework provides the proper **quantum field theory formulation** for phononic excitations. The twisted Poincaré invariance ensures consistency of the effective phonon field theory.
