# Fedele Lizzi: Complete Research Library (30 papers)

## Overview

Fedele Lizzi is the primary developer of **zeta function spectral action**, the renormalizable alternative to the standard cutoff formulation. His work spans:

1. **Spectral Action Foundations** (Papers 01-04): How to properly regularize the action using zeta functions
2. **Renormalizability & UV Behavior** (Papers 05-07): Why cutoff fails, what zeta fixes, coupling unification
3. **Extended Sectors & Phenomenology** (Papers 08-11): Hidden scalar fields, Standard Model completion
4. **Lorentz Signature & Fermions** (Papers 10, 13): How Euclidean formulation connects to physical spacetime
5. **Noncommutative Spacetime** (Papers 12-27): κ-deformed and ρ-Minkowski geometry, quantum groups
6. **Philosophical Foundations** (Paper 28): Basho (place-theory) interpretation of quantum geometry
7. **Experimental & Phenomenological** (Papers 29-30): Recent neutrino physics and operator-ordering theory
