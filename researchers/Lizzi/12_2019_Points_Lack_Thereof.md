# Points. Lack thereof.

**Authors:** Fedele Lizzi
**Year:** 2019
**arXiv:** 1905.01653v2

---

## Abstract

Philosophical and technical examination of what "points" mean in noncommutative geometry. At Planck scales, the notion of a geometric point loses meaning. Using spectral action formalism with high-momenta expansions, we show that bosonic propagators connecting different points vanish at extreme UV scales, suggesting spacetime becomes "non-local" at Planck distance.

---

## Key Arguments

### 1. Traditional Point Concept
- Classical geometry: spacetime = points with metric g_μν
- Differential geometry: tangent spaces, curvature, geodesics
- Quantum gravity suspicion: points may not be fundamental at Planck scale

### 2. Noncommutativity vs. Points
- Noncommutative geometry: position coordinates [x^μ, x^ν] ≠ 0
- Uncertainty principle for space: Δx ≥ ℓ_P (Planck length)
- Implication: cannot simultaneously specify position and momentum arbitrarily well

### 3. Spectral Action at High Momenta
From Paper 05 (High energy bosons), at p >> Λ:

**Propagator structure**:
- Scalar: $D_H(p^2) \sim 1/p^4$
- Gauge boson: $D_V(p^2) \sim 1/p^4$

These **do not decrease** with increasing p; instead, they oscillate/diverge.

### 4. Absence of Point-to-Point Connections
The propagator describes amplitude for a field to propagate from spacetime point x to point y:

$$P(x,y) \propto \int d^4p \, e^{ip(x-y)} D_V(p^2)$$

At **very high momenta** (UV regime), the absence of proper propagator decrease means:

- For distances Δx ~ ℓ_P, integrand oscillates wildly
- Net amplitude → 0 due to destructive interference
- **Points separated by Planck distance cannot communicate**

This is NOT a quantum effect (no ℏ involved) but a **purely spectral geometry effect**.

### 5. Operational Perspective
In QFT, we cannot "probe" points smaller than:

$$\Delta x_{\min} \sim \sqrt{\frac{\text{Planck length}^2 + (\text{spectral length scale})^2}}{\text{}}$$

Where spectral length scale ~ 1/Λ (cutoff scale of spectral action).

At Planck scale, resolution becomes impossible—"points" become indistinguishable.

## Key Results

1. **Emergence of non-locality**: Below Planck scale, points are not well-defined as distinguishable entities.

2. **Spectral geometry provides UV cutoff**: The spectral structure naturally provides a minimum observable length scale.

3. **Geometric picture changes**: Spacetime cannot be visualized as a manifold of distinguishable points below ℓ_P.

4. **Connection to holography**: The lack of point-to-point communication suggests spacetime might be a **holographic projection** of higher-dimensional theory (speculative).

---

## Philosophical Implications

- Spacetime is **emergent**, not fundamental
- "Points" are useful macroscopic fiction above Planck scale
- Fundamental degrees of freedom are **holographic** or purely algebraic
- Quantum gravity naturally incorporates "no points" feature
