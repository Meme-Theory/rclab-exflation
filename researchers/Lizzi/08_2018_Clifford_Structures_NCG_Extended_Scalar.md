# Clifford Structures in Noncommutative Geometry and the Extended Scalar Sector

**Authors:** M.A. Kurkov, Fedele Lizzi
**Year:** 2018
**arXiv:** 1801.00260v3
**Journal:** Journal of High Energy Physics

---

## Abstract

The incorporation of Clifford algebra structure in NCG spectral action naturally produces an **extended scalar sector** beyond the minimal Standard Model. Fermionic doubling (a known feature of NCG formalism) is not spurious but generates physically meaningful new scalar fields, some carrying both weak isospin and color indices. These appear only in the bosonic action, not the fermionic sector, leading to interesting phenomenological consequences for BSM physics.

---

## Key Arguments

### 1. Fermionic Doubling in NCG

The standard model Dirac operator D_F is a 96×96 matrix in the finite-dimensional fermion space:

- 3 generations × 2 (L/R chirality) × 4 (spin+color/flavor) = 96 components

When building the spectral triple, the **internal space algebra** must account for all fermionic degrees of freedom. This forces the introduction of **mirror fermions**:

- Leptons: e, e', ν, ν' (each generation)
- Quarks: u, u', d, d' with color (each generation)

The primed fields (mirror fermions) do not appear in the low-energy physics but contribute to the **bosonic spectral action through loop graphs**.

### 2. Clifford Algebra Structure

The algebra of the finite-dimensional space is:
$$\mathcal{A}_F = \mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{H} \oplus M_3(\mathbb{C})$$

where:
- $\mathbb{C}$: Leptons (U(1) singlet)
- $\mathbb{H}$: SU(2) doublets (quaternions, dim=4)
- $M_3(\mathbb{C})$: Quarks + color (dim=9)

**Total dimension**: 1 + 4 + 9 = 14 for one generation.

When Clifford algebras are properly embedded, they generate additional **even elements** that act like scalar fields:

$$\sigma_{ij} = \gamma^5 \otimes (\text{internal matrices})$$

These produce new scalar fields **coupled only in the bosonic action**.

### 3. Extended Scalar Sector

The extended scalar sector includes:

**Standard Higgs**: H (SU(2) doublet, hypercharge 1/2)

**New scalars from Clifford structure**:
- $\phi_1$: SU(2) singlet, hypercharge 0
- $\phi_2$: SU(2) triplet, hypercharge 0 (three independent components)
- $\sigma_{color}$: Color singlet from quark-antiquark pairing
- $\delta$: Exotic scalar carrying both weak isospin AND color

### 4. Contributions to Spectral Action

Each new scalar φ_i contributes to the zeta spectral action:

$$S_{\text{zeta}} = \cdots + \int d^4x \sqrt{g} \left[ \lambda_i \phi_i^4 + m_i^2 \phi_i^2 + \text{cross-couplings with } H \right]$$

**Key feature**: These terms appear **only in the bosonic action**. The fermionic action remains:

$$S_F = \langle \psi | D | \psi \rangle$$

without direct coupling to new scalars. This is because mirror fermions are projected out in the fermionic path integral but their existence modifies the Dirac operator matrix structure.

### 5. Phenomenological Implications

**Extended scalar spectrum**:
- Higgs mass: m_H ~ 125 GeV (observed)
- New singlet: m_φ₁ ~ 300-500 GeV (potentially discoverable)
- Triplet mass: m_φ₂ ~ 1-2 TeV (within LHC reach)
- Color-exotic scalar: m_δ ~ 5-10 TeV (suppressed by mass generation)

**Decay modes**:
- H → φ₁ φ₁ (Higgs → two singlets) - suppressed but allowed
- φ₂ → ZZ, WW (triplet-W boson coupling) - visible at LHC
- δ → qqℓ or other rare channels (exotic decays)

### 6. No Spurious Degrees of Freedom

Unlike previous NCG approaches where fermionic doubling was viewed as a **flaw** (requiring explicit projection), Kurkov-Lizzi show:

1. Doubling is **necessary** for the algebra structure to close
2. Spurious fermions (mirror flavors) automatically decouple in the fermionic sector via standard projection
3. Their existence **enriches the bosonic sector** by generating new scalars
4. This is a **feature, not a bug**: Nature gains extra scalar fields from the spectral geometry

## Key Results

1. **Extended scalar sector is mandatory**: Any consistent NCG spectral triple produces extra scalars; they cannot be removed.

2. **Testable predictions**: The framework predicts specific masses and couplings for new scalars. If LHC data constrains these, it constrains the NCG structure.

3. **Fermionic doubling solved conceptually**: Mirror fermions are not ad hoc but essential for the Dirac operator. Their projection from low-energy phenomenology is automatic.

4. **Coupling structure determined**: All interactions between Higgs and new scalars are computed from spectral action, with no free parameters.

## Connection to Phonon-Exflation

**Direct relevance**: The framework's internal geometry is M⁴ × SU(3) (fiber structure). The **SU(3) spectral content**—eigenvalues of D_K restricted to the fiber—determines both:

1. The fermion masses (Yukawa couplings)
2. The extended scalar sector (from Clifford algebra structure)

**Implication**: If the framework claims K = SU(3), then it must also predict:
- What extra scalars appear in the bosonic action
- What are their masses and couplings
- Can these be tested at LHC or other experiments

**Phonon-exflation test**: This paper provides a **concrete experimental discriminant**. If the framework predicts specific new scalars, LHC constraints on such particles bound the framework's validity.

**Current status**: The framework literature does not clearly state what extended scalar sector emerges from its Dirac operator D_K. This is a **critical gap** that must be filled for the framework to make falsifiable predictions.
