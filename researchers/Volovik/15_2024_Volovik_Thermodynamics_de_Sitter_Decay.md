## Abstract

We discuss the consequences of the unique symmetry of the de Sitter spacetime. This symmetry leads to the specific thermodynamic properties of the de Sitter vacuum, which produces the thermal bath for matter. The de Sitter spacetime is invariant under the modified translations, $\mathbf{r} \to \mathbf{r} - e^{Ht}\mathbf{a}$, where $H$ is the Hubble parameter. For $H \to 0$, this symmetry corresponds to the conventional invariance of Minkowski spacetime under translations $\mathbf{r} \to \mathbf{r} - \mathbf{a}$. Due to this symmetry, all the comoving observers at any point of the de Sitter space perceive the de Sitter environment as the thermal bath with temperature $T = H/\pi$, which is twice larger than the Gibbons-Hawking temperature of the cosmological horizon. This temperature does not violate the de Sitter symmetry, and thus does not require the preferred reference frame. This leads to the heat exchange between gravity and matter, and to instability of de Sitter state towards the creation of matter, its further heating, and finally to the decay of the de Sitter state.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### II. de Sitter as Heat Bath
Using the Painleve-Gullstrand metric $ds^2 = -dt^2 + (dr - Hr\,dt)^2$, Volovik shows that an atom at the origin can be ionized by the de Sitter gravitational field. The WKB tunneling rate gives:

$$w \sim \exp\left(-\frac{\pi E_n}{H}\right) = \exp\left(-\frac{E_n}{T}\right), \quad T = H/\pi$$

This temperature is twice the Gibbons-Hawking temperature $T_{GH} = H/2\pi$. The factor of 2 arises because Hawking radiation involves coherent co-tunneling of two particles across the horizon, while ionization is a single-particle local process.

### III. Local Thermodynamics
The vacuum energy density $\epsilon_{\text{vac}} = 3H^2/(8\pi G) = 3\pi T^2/(8G)$ is quadratic in temperature, yielding linear entropy density:

$$s_{\text{vac}} = \frac{3\pi}{4G} T = 12\pi^2 K T$$

This is identical to the Sommerfeld law for a Fermi liquid. The total entropy of the Hubble volume reproduces the Gibbons-Hawking area law: $S_{\text{bulk}} = s_{\text{vac}} V_H = A/(4G) = S_{\text{hor}}$.

### IV. Multi-metric Gravity and Thermalization
Considering an ensemble of $N$ sub-Universes with different $H_n$, heat exchange equilibrates them to a common Hubble parameter, confirming $T = H/\pi$ as the thermodynamic temperature. This parallels magnon BEC formation from incoherent precessions.

### V. f(R) Gravity Extension
In $f(R)$ gravity, the effective gravitational coupling $K = df/dR$ and curvature $R$ are thermodynamically conjugate variables (like $B$ and $H$ in magnetism). The holographic correspondence $S_{\text{bulk}} = 4\pi K A = S_{\text{hor}}$ holds with $K = df/dR$.

### VI. de Sitter Decay
The de Sitter state decays by thermalizing matter. The fermion triplication process $e \to e + e\bar{e}$ at rate $\Gamma \sim \exp(-2m/T)$ leads to exponential particle creation ($3^n$ fermions after $n$ replications), demonstrating de Sitter instability.

### VII-VIII. Black Hole Thermodynamics
The gravastar (black hole with de Sitter core) connects de Sitter and black hole thermodynamics. The contracting de Sitter ($H < 0$) has negative temperature and negative entropy. Black hole entropy arises from the shrinking of the negative-entropy de Sitter core during gravastar relaxation.

## Key Results

1. Local de Sitter temperature $T = H/\pi = 2T_{GH}$ from WKB tunneling, independent of horizon
2. de Sitter vacuum has Fermi-liquid-like thermodynamics: $s \propto T$, $\epsilon \propto T^2$
3. Bulk-surface holographic correspondence $S_{\text{bulk}} = A/(4G)$ holds only in 3+1 dimensions
4. $K$ and $R$ are thermodynamically conjugate in $f(R)$ gravity
5. de Sitter is unstable: matter creation via triplication leads to exponential decay
6. In 3+1d uniquely: $[K] = [R] = 1/l^2$, enabling the holographic correspondence
7. Cosmological constant problem solved thermodynamically: $\rho_{\text{vac}} = 0$ at $T = 0$, $P = 0$
