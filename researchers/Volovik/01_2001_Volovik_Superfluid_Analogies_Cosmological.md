## Abstract

In a modern viewpoint the relativistic quantum field theory is the emergent phenomenon arising in the low energy corner of the physical fermionic vacuum -- the medium, whose nature remains unknown. The same phenomenon occurs in the condensed matter systems: In the extreme limit of low energy the condensed matter system of special universality class acquires all the symmetries, which we know today in high energy physics: Lorentz invariance, gauge invariance, general covariance, etc. The chiral fermions as well as gauge bosons and gravity field arise as fermionic and bosonic collective modes of the system. The inhomogeneous states of the condensed matter ground state -- vacuum -- induce nontrivial effective metrics of the space, where the free quasiparticles move along geodesics. This conceptual similarity between condensed matter and quantum vacuum allows us to simulate many phenomena in high energy physics and cosmology, including axial anomaly, baryoproduction and magnetogenesis, event horizon and Hawking radiation, cosmological constant and rotating vacuum, etc., probing these phenomena in ultra-low-temperature superfluid helium, atomic Bose condensates and superconductors. Some of the experiments have been already conducted.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### I. Introduction: Physical Vacuum as Condensed Matter

Volovik contrasts two views of symmetry: (1) the Grand Unification view where higher energy = higher symmetry, broken at low energy; (2) the "anti-grand-unification" view where symmetries EMERGE at low energy from an underlying medium without those symmetries. Superfluid 3He-A exemplifies both: below T_c, symmetries break (Grand Unification scheme), but as T approaches 0, the system ACQUIRES Lorentz invariance, gauge invariance, and elements of general covariance from nothing. This happens because 3He-A belongs to a special universality class of Fermi systems.

The detailed information on the system is lost in the acoustic/hydrodynamic limit. From the properties of low-energy collective modes one cannot reconstruct the atomic structure. The naive approach to calculate the ground state vacuum energy using zero-point energy of phonons gives even the wrong sign of the vacuum energy.

### II. Landau-Khalatnikov Two-Fluid Hydrodynamics as Effective Theory of Gravity

The superfluid vacuum dynamics is characterized by two hydrodynamic variables: particle number density n(r,t) and superfluid velocity v_s(r,t). The system has two subsystems: the superfluid condensate (vacuum) and quasiparticles (matter), constituting the two-fluid model.

The quadratic Lagrangian for sound waves has necessarily the Lorentzian form. Phonons propagate in an effective acoustic metric:

g^{00} = -1/(mnc), g^{0i} = -v_s^i/(mnc), g^{ij} = (c^2 delta^{ij} - v_s^i v_s^j)/(mnc)

This is the acoustic metric first identified by Unruh. The effective Riemann metric is simulated by the smooth parts of the hydrodynamic fields.

### Vacuum Energy and Cosmological Constant (Section II.G)

This is the CRITICAL section for the phonon-exflation framework. Volovik shows that the vacuum energy density and vacuum pressure are related as in the Einstein cosmological term:

epsilon_vac(equilibrium) = -P_vac

For a self-sustaining system (liquid in equilibrium without environment), P_vac = 0, therefore:

epsilon_vac(self-sustaining system) = 0

This is the NULLIFICATION of vacuum energy. The key insight: from the SOLE assumption that the underlying physical vacuum is liquid (self-sustaining system), the energy of the vacuum in equilibrium at T=0 is identically zero, independent of microscopic details. The trans-Planckian degrees of freedom cancel the zero-point energy contribution exactly.

The effective theory estimation of vacuum energy violates this zero condition. Moreover, it gives the wrong sign. This means the effective theory must be used with great caution for quantities that depend on the Planck energy scale.

### IV. Fermi Point Topology and Standard Model

Systems with Fermi points are topologically characterized by the invariant:

N_3 = (1/24pi^2) epsilon^{mu nu lambda} tr integral dS_sigma G partial_mu G^{-1} G partial_nu G^{-1} G partial_lambda G^{-1}

Near a Fermi point, the inverse propagator takes the universal form G^{-1} = sigma^a e^mu_a (p_mu - p^(0)_mu), giving rise to the Lorentzian metric g^{mu nu} = eta^{ab} e^mu_a e^nu_b. This means in the vicinity of any Fermi point, quasiparticles are ALWAYS described by a Lorentzian metric, even if the underlying system is non-Lorentz invariant.

### V. Fermi Points: 3He-A vs Standard Model

In 3He-A, the four chiral fermionic species are organized as left-handed and right-handed weak doublets. The triad (e_1, e_2, l-hat) of the order parameter gives rise to the vierbein/tetrad. The gauge field A = p_F l-hat. The speeds of light are c_perp = Delta_0/p_F and c_parallel = v_F = p_F/m*.

The Standard Model with G(224) = SU(2)_L x SU(2)_R x SU(4)_C has 16 fermions per generation with the same topological structure as the Fermi-point universality class.

### VII. Chiral Anomaly and Baryogenesis

The Adler-Bell-Jackiw equation is derived from the spectral flow in the presence of Fermi points. The anomalous nucleation of fermionic charge in 3He-A is the direct analog of baryogenesis in the electroweak vacuum. The rate of particle production is:

n-dot = (1/4pi^2)(e_R^2 - e_L^2) E . B

### X. Vacuum Pressure in Superfluid 3He

The interplay between vacuum pressure and matter pressure is demonstrated. The cosmological term in 3He: the vacuum energy compensates matter pressure to give zero total pressure in equilibrium. The vacuum energy is non-zero only when the vacuum is perturbed (by matter, curvature, or rotation).

### XII-XIII. Event Horizon and Hawking Radiation

The effective metric in moving superfluid creates event horizons when the flow velocity exceeds the local speed of sound. The Hawking temperature is T_H = (hbar/2pi) dv_s/dr evaluated at the horizon.

### XIV. How to Improve Helium-3

Volovik discusses how to construct a non-superfluid condensed matter system in the same universality class as 3He-A that could better simulate Einstein gravity without contamination from superfluid kinetic energy terms.
