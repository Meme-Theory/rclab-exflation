## Abstract

We consider the discrete $\mathbb{Z}_4$ symmetry $\hat{i}$, which takes place in the scenario of quantum gravity where the gravitational tetrads emerge as the order parameter -- the vacuum expectation value of the bilinear combination of fermionic operators. Under this symmetry operation $\hat{i}$, the emerging tetrads are multiplied by the imaginary unit, $\hat{i} e^a_\mu = -i e^a_\mu$. The existence of such symmetry and the spontaneous breaking of this symmetry are also supported by the consideration of the symmetry breaking scheme in the topological superfluid $^3$He-B.

---

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### Composite Tetrads and $\hat{i}$-symmetry
In the Akama-Diakonov-Wetterich (ADW) approach, gravitational tetrads emerge as composite objects from fermionic fields:

$$\hat{E}^a_\mu = \frac{1}{2}\left(\Psi^\dagger \gamma^a \partial_\mu \Psi - \Psi^\dagger \overleftarrow{\partial}_\mu \gamma^a \Psi\right)$$

The cosmological term action $S = \frac{1}{24} e_{\alpha\beta\mu\nu} e_{abcd} \int d^4x \, \hat{E}^a_\alpha \hat{E}^b_\beta \hat{E}^c_\mu \hat{E}^d_\nu$ is invariant under $x^\mu \to ix^\mu$.

### Broken $\hat{i}$-symmetry
The tetrad order parameter $e^a_\mu = \langle \hat{E}^a_\mu \rangle$ breaks:
- Separate Lorentz symmetries: $L_L \times L_S \to L_J$
- The $\hat{i}$-symmetry: $\hat{i} e^a_\mu = -i e^a_\mu$

Since $\hat{i}^2 e^a_\mu = -e^a_\mu$ corresponds to $PT$, the $\hat{i}$-operation is the square root of $PT$-symmetry, forming $\mathbb{Z}_4 = (\hat{i}, \hat{i}^2 = PT, \hat{i}^3 = -\hat{i}, \hat{i}^4 = 1)$.

### Consequences
- Metric changes signature: $\hat{i} g_{\mu\nu} = -g_{\mu\nu}$
- Interval is $\hat{i}$-invariant: $\hat{i}(ds^2) = ds^2$
- Gauge 1-forms transform: $\hat{i} A_\mu = -iA_\mu$
- Einstein-Hilbert action and Dirac action are both $\hat{i}$-invariant
- Domain walls between Hermitian and anti-Hermitian tetrads ("H-antiH walls")

### $^3$He-B Analogy
In $^3$He-B, the symmetry breaking is $U(1) \times SO(3)_L \times SO(3)_S \to SO(3)_J$, making the triads complex. The $\hat{i}$-operation corresponds to $U(1)$ phase rotation by $\pi/2$.

### Origin of $i = \sqrt{-1}$
The imaginary unit can be expressed as a real $2 \times 2$ matrix: $\hat{i}_{\text{eff}} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. The $\mathbb{Z}_4$ symmetry may extend to the full $U(1)$ of quantum mechanics, as happens in the planar phase of $^3$He.

## Key Results

1. ADW quantum gravity has $\mathbb{Z}_4$ discrete symmetry under $x^\mu \to ix^\mu$
2. Spontaneous breaking produces H-antiH domain walls
3. The $\hat{i}$-symmetry is the square root of $PT$
4. The imaginary unit in quantum mechanics may be emergent from topology
5. In the alternative scenario, $\hat{i}R = -R$ and $K = 1/(16\pi G)$ is the order parameter
6. Vestigial gravity (metric without tetrads) violates equivalence principle
