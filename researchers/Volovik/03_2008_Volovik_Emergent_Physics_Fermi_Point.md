## Abstract

The Fermi-point scenario of emergent gravity has the following consequences: gravity emerges together with fermionic and bosonic matter; emergent fermionic matter consists of massless Weyl fermions; emergent bosonic matter consists of gauge fields; Lorentz symmetry persists well above the Planck energy; space-time is naturally 4-dimensional; Universe is naturally flat; cosmological constant is naturally small or zero; underlying physics is based on discrete symmetries; 'quantum gravity' cannot be obtained by quantization of Einstein equations; there is no contradiction between quantum mechanics and gravity; etc.

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### 1. Introduction: The Hierarchy and CC Problems

The cosmological constant problem: Lambda_obs ~ (10^{-3} eV)^4 vs naive Lambda_theor ~ E_P^4 with E_P ~ 10^{19} GeV. The hierarchy problem: observed particle masses M_obs < E_ew ~ 1 TeV vs naive M_theor ~ E_P. Volovik argues both are resolved by the same principle: emergent physics based on momentum-space topology.

### 2. Fermionic and Bosonic Content from Fermi Points

The key result from the Atiyah-Bott-Shapiro construction: near an isolated Fermi point with topological charge N = +/-1, the inverse propagator takes the universal form:

G^{-1}(p_mu) = e^beta_alpha Gamma^alpha (p_beta - p^{(0)}_beta) + higher order terms

where Gamma^mu = (1, sigma_x, sigma_y, sigma_z) are Pauli matrices, p^{(0)}_beta is the position of the Fermi point, and e^beta_alpha is the tetrad matrix.

**(a) Emergent fermions**: The topological stability of the Fermi point ensures the spectrum remains gapless under arbitrary perturbations. This solves the hierarchy problem: masses are naturally zero (or small).

**(b) Emergent gauge fields**: The vector p^{(0)}_beta, which shifts the Fermi point position, acts as a U(1) gauge field A_beta. For higher-charge Fermi points (|N|>1), the shift becomes a matrix field giving non-Abelian gauge fields. N=2 with Z_2 symmetry gives SU(2).

**(c) Emergent gravity**: The matrix e^k_i acts as the vierbein field, describing emergent dynamical gravity. Gravity emerges TOGETHER WITH matter -- they cannot be separated at the microscopic level. This means "quantum gravity" must be the unified theory of the underlying quantum vacuum.

**(d) Dimension of spacetime**: The Atiyah-Bott-Shapiro construction naturally gives D=4 (or D=8) spacetime dimensions, because the Clifford algebra of Dirac matrices Gamma^a (a=0,1,...,D-1) has a fundamental representation of dimension 2^{D/2} which must match the matrix size.

### 3. Vacuum Energy and Cosmological Constant

**(a) Vacuum as self-sustained medium**: The vacuum is characterized by a conserved tensor charge q_{mu nu} satisfying nabla_mu q^{mu nu} = 0. In homogeneous vacuum, q_{mu nu} = q g_{mu nu}. The vacuum is a thermodynamic system with energy epsilon(q), volume V, and total charge Q = qV.

The Gibbs-Duhem relation gives the vacuum pressure:
P_vac = -epsilon(q) + q d epsilon/dq

For a self-sustained vacuum (no external environment, P=0):
Lambda = -P_vac = 0

The cosmological constant is NATURALLY zero. The huge contribution of zero-point energy is cancelled by trans-Planckian degrees of freedom (Figure 3 in paper).

**(d) Compressibility of the vacuum**: chi^{-1}_vac = q^2 d^2 epsilon/dq^2 > 0 (stability condition). The compressibility is a fundamental constant alongside G, both determined by Planck physics.

**(e) Thermal fluctuations**: Mean square fluctuations of Lambda are <(Delta Lambda)^2> = T/(V chi_vac), giving a lower bound on the volume of the Universe.

### 4. Hierarchy of Energy Scales

CRITICAL: Volovik argues Lorentz invariance must persist WELL ABOVE E_Planck. Two UV cutoffs exist:
- E_Planck: the composite (bosonic) scale
- E_Lorentz >> E_P: the atomic scale of the quantum vacuum

When E_Lorentz >> E_P, Einstein equations emerge. When E_Lorentz << E_P, hydrodynamic (Euler) equations govern the metric. Experimental bounds suggest E_Lorentz > 10^9 E_P.

The number of microscopic constituents N ~ V/l^3_Lorentz ~ 10^{270}, making the Universe a macroscopic thermodynamic system.

### 5. Discrete Symmetries

The underlying physics is based on discrete symmetries rather than continuous ones. Fermion zero modes and topological invariants in 3He-A are protected by the discrete symmetry K = U_{SU2} x C (product of a SU(2) spin rotation and charge conjugation). The Standard Model may similarly emerge from discrete symmetries.
