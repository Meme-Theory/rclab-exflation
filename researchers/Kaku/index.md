## Topic Map

### A. String Field Theory Foundations
Papers: 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 11, 14
The original Kaku-Kikkawa program: second-quantize the string as a multilocal field, derive Veneziano amplitudes from trees, prove finiteness from loops, unify light-cone and covariant formulations via the string vielbein, and extend to closed strings (nonpolynomial, gravity included). Paper 14 synthesizes 35+ years of this program. This is the most technically dense and framework-relevant cluster.

### B. Conformal Field Theory and Compactification
Papers: 06, 08
CFT as the worldsheet language of strings. Central charge, modular invariance, Calabi-Yau compactification, vertex operators, orbifolds, mirror symmetry, WZW models, Yang-Baxter equations and quantum groups. Paper 08 is the advanced treatment including topological field theory.

### C. Superstring Phenomenology
Papers: 09, 19
How the Standard Model gauge group emerges from string compactification. Coupling constant unification at the GUT scale, threshold corrections from massive string modes, Higgs mechanism and fermion masses, asymptotic freedom, anomaly cancellation. Paper 19 is the complete SM gauge theory exposition.

### D. M-Theory and Dualities
Papers: 07, 10, 15, 16, 17
The five superstring theories as limits of 11D M-theory. T-duality (R <-> alpha'/R), S-duality (g_s <-> 1/g_s), U-duality combining both. Type IIA/IIB mirror symmetry, 11D supergravity as the low-energy limit, membrane and M5-brane solutions, BPS states. Paper 07 is the comprehensive graduate textbook.

### E. Extra Dimensions and Kaluza-Klein
Papers: 12, 23
The KK mechanism: 5D gravity yields 4D gravity + electromagnetism. KK towers, moduli stabilization, Randall-Sundrum warped geometry solving the hierarchy problem, neutrino masses from bulk fermions, KK dark matter candidates. Paper 23 is the systematic technical treatment.

### F. Cosmology and Landscape
Papers: 20, 21, 22
The string landscape (~10^500 vacua), KKLT moduli stabilization, the eta problem obstructing slow-roll inflation, eternal inflation and bubble nucleation, the measure problem, anthropic selection. Paper 21 is the most technically substantive.

### G. Swampland and Quantum Gravity Constraints
Papers: 29, 30
The swampland program: distance conjecture, weak gravity condition, gradient bound forbidding flat potentials, de Sitter instability. Direct implications for ruling out classes of effective field theories. Paper 30 synthesizes the entire unification quest.

### H. Grand Unification Survey
Papers: 27, 28
Comparative analysis of string theory, LQG, twistors, causal sets, asymptotic safety, NCG spectral approaches. Five criteria for the "God Equation." Paper 28 includes the most detailed treatment of NCG unification.

### I. Quantum Field Theory Foundations
Papers: 18
Path integral formulation, Feynman diagrams, generating functionals, Ward-Takahashi identities, gauge fixing with Faddeev-Popov ghosts, instantons, asymptotic freedom. Foundation for all perturbative and non-perturbative calculations in the collection.

### J. Speculative Physics
Papers: 13, 24, 25, 26
Kardashev civilizations, quantum consciousness debate, wormholes, warp drives, teleportation. Grounded in known physics but speculative in application. Lowest direct relevance to the computational framework.

- **File**: `01_1974_Kaku_Kikkawa_LightCone_StringFieldTheory.md`
- **arXiv**: N/A (pre-arXiv)
- **Year**: 1974
- **Relevance**: CRITICAL
- **Tags**: string field theory, light-cone quantization, Veneziano amplitude, multilocal field, second quantization

**Summary**: The founding paper of string field theory. Kaku and Kikkawa perform canonical quantization of relativistic strings as multilocal fields Phi[x(sigma)] in light-cone gauge, derive three-string and four-string interaction vertices, recover the Veneziano amplitude from s- and t-channel tree sums, and show that Yang-Mills theory emerges in the zero-slope limit alpha' -> 0.

- Light-cone gauge is ghost-free with positive-definite Hilbert space
- Infinite Regge trajectories emerge from string oscillator modes: M^2 ~ 2n/alpha'
- Yang-Mills is the alpha' -> 0 limit of SFT
- Four-string contact vertex is necessary to avoid spurious poles
