# The APS-Index and the Spectral Flow

**Author(s):** Koen van den Dungen, Lennart Ronge
**Year:** 2020
**Journal:** Operators and Matrices 15, No. 4 (2021), 1393–1416
**arXiv:** 2004.01085

---

## Abstract

We examine the Atiyah-Patodi-Singer (APS) index and prove its equality to spectral flow in a functional analytic framework. We study families of self-adjoint Fredholm operators parametrized over finite intervals. In the Riemannian case, $D := \frac{d}{dt}+A$, and the Lorentzian case, $D := \frac{d}{dt}-iA$, we establish that the Fredholm index of the operator D equipped with APS boundary conditions equals the spectral flow of the family A(t).

---

## Key Results

1. **APS Index = Spectral Flow**: The Fredholm index of $\frac{d}{dt} ± A$ with APS boundary conditions equals the spectral flow of A(t).

2. **Lorentzian Extension**: Generalizes Bär-Strohmaier results to indefinite-signature settings.

3. **Boundary Term Cancellation**: The APS eta-invariant boundary term exactly cancels when computing indices, leaving only the spectral flow.
