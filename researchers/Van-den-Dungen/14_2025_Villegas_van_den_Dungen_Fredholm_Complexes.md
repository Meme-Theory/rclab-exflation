# Fredholm Complexes of Hilbert C*-modules

**Author(s):** Brian Villegas-Villalpando, Koen van den Dungen
**Year:** 2025
**Journal:** [Submitted] Operator Algebras and K-Theory
**arXiv:** 2505.07568

---

## Abstract

We investigate cochain complexes of Hilbert C*-modules with unbounded regular operators as differential maps. We provide various equivalent characterizations of the Fredholm property for such complexes and establish a Fredholm index valued in K-theory groups of the underlying C*-algebra. The index demonstrates stability under small or relatively compact perturbations. We provide alternative expressions for computing the index when weak or strong Hodge decompositions exist.

---

## Key Results

1. **Fredholm Complexes**: Define Fredholm property for unbounded operator complexes on C*-modules—generalizing Fredholm operators to complex sequences.

2. **Index Valued in K-Theory**: The Fredholm index takes values in K₀(A) for the underlying C*-algebra A—a topological invariant.

3. **Stability Under Perturbation**: Index is robust to small and relatively compact perturbations.

4. **Hodge Decomposition**: When Hodge decomposition exists (harmonic + exact + coexact), the index computes via ker/coker of harmonic operator.
