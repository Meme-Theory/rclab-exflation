# Krein Spectral Triples and the Fermionic Action

**Author(s):** Koen van den Dungen
**Year:** 2015
**Journal:** Mathematical Physics, Analysis and Geometry 19 (2016)
**arXiv:** 1505.01939

---

## Abstract

We introduce Krein spectral triples as a generalization of standard spectral triples, extending from Hilbert spaces to Krein spaces. This framework allows for an improved formulation of the fermionic action for almost-commutative manifolds. We demonstrate that this Krein space approach successfully recovers correct Lagrangians for electrodynamics, electro-weak theory, and the Standard Model. Importantly, the formulation does not require a real structure, unless one includes Majorana masses.

---

## Key Concepts

**Krein Spaces**: A generalization of Hilbert spaces where the inner product is indefinite: ⟨ψ, φ⟩_J = ⟨ψ, Jφ⟩ for a self-adjoint involution J. This allows treatment of both positive and negative frequency modes, essential for quantum field theory.

**Fermionic Action**: The kinetic term for fermions in the Standard Model Lagrangian:
$$S_{ferm} = ∫ d^4x \overline{ψ} (iγ^μ D_μ) ψ$$

In NCG, this is naturally recovered from the trace of the Dirac operator squared over the fermionic degrees of freedom, but only in the Krein space formulation.

**Key Results**:

1. **Fermionic Kinetic Terms**: The Krein spectral triple framework correctly produces the minimal coupling of fermions to gauge fields.

2. **No Real Structure Required**: Unlike earlier treatments requiring an involutive "real structure" J₀ on the spectral triple, Krein spaces allow fermionic actions without it (except for Majorana/CP-invariant terms).

3. **Correct Chirality Handling**: The framework naturally separates left- and right-handed fermions, correctly implementing parity violation in the weak interaction.

4. **Standard Model Recovery**: The complete Standard Model fermionic sector is recovered: Dirac leptons, Weyl neutrinos, and CKM mixing.

## Connection to Framework

**METHODOLOGICAL IMPORTANCE**: The fermionic sector in phonon-exflation is built on the Krein space formalism because:

1. **BCS Pairing**: The BCS condensate involves particle-hole mixing, which is naturally formulated in Krein spaces (creation and annihilation operators with indefinite metric).

2. **Fermionic Quantization**: Quasiparticles (phonons) emerging from the condensate have indefinite-metric structure—correctly captured by Krein spectral triples.

3. **Non-Relativistic Limit**: When the framework is extended to nuclear/condensed matter systems (Volovik analogs), the Krein space structure enables non-relativistic fermions.

4. **Real Structure**: In phonon-exflation, the choice of real structure encodes the particle-antiparticle conjugation symmetry of the internal SU(3) fiber, essential for correct baryon number conservation.
