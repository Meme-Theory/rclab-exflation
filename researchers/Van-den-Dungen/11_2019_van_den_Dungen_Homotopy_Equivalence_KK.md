# Homotopy Equivalence in Unbounded KK-Theory

**Author(s):** Koen van den Dungen, Bram Mesland
**Year:** 2019
**Journal:** Annals of K-Theory 5, No. 3 (2020), 489–518
**arXiv:** 1907.04049

---

## Abstract

We introduce a generalized unbounded KK-cycle concept extending Kasparov modules and ensuring the direct sum is well-defined. We establish that homotopy equivalence classes form an abelian group structure $\overline{UKK}(A,B)$ for σ-unital C*-algebras. For separable algebras A, our group $\overline{UKK}(A,B)$ is isomorphic to Kasparov's classical KK-theory group KK(A,B) via the bounded transform.

---

## Key Results

1. **Generalized Unbounded Cycles**: New notion of unbounded KK-cycle with well-defined direct sums, extending Kasparov modules beyond their standard domain.

2. **Abelian Group Structure**: Homotopy equivalence classes form an abelian group, enabling systematic composition of cycles.

3. **Isomorphism to Classical KK**: For separable algebras, $\overline{UKK}(A,B) ≅ KK(A,B)$—unbounded and bounded KK-theory are equivalent.

4. **Homotopy Characterization**: Homotopy relations on unbounded cycles align with operator-homotopy plus degenerate cycles.
