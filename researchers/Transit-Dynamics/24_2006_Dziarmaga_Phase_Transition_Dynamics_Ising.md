# Dynamics of a Quantum Phase Transition

**Author(s):** Jacek Dziarmaga
**Year:** 2005-2010
**Journal:** Physical Review B, Physical Review Letters

---

## Abstract

Dziarmaga provided exact solutions for the dynamics of quantum phase transitions in the transverse-field Ising model. When the system is driven through the critical point at finite rate, the ground-state adiabatic condition is violated. Excitations are created with density scaling as (quench rate)^{−α}, where α depends on critical exponents.

---

## Key Results

1. **Exact Scaling**: For Ising model, defect density ~ √{(dλ/dt)} (square-root scaling).

2. **Kibble-Zurek Mechanism Verified**: Predictions match numerical simulations to high precision.

3. **Beyond KZM**: Extensions to non-linear quenches, periodic drives (Floquet systems).
