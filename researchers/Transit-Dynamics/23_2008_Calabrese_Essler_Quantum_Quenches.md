# Quantum Quenches in Transverse-Field Ising Chain

**Author(s):** Pasquale Calabrese, Fabian H. L. Essler, Maurizio Fagotti
**Year:** 2011-2012 (key papers)
**Journal:** Physical Review Letters, Journal of Statistical Mechanics

---

## Abstract

Calabrese, Essler, and Fagotti solved the quantum-quench problem in the transverse-field Ising model exactly. After a sudden parameter quench from H_i to H_f, the system evolves to a steady state described by the GGE (generalized Gibbs ensemble) on an integrable island, or to thermal equilibrium if integrability is broken.

---

## Key Results

1. **Exact GGE Steady State**: Derived exact occupation numbers and two-point functions for the steady state.

2. **Entanglement Entropy Saturation**: S_A reaches a value set by the number of conserved charges; no further growth.

3. **Applicability**: Methods extend to many integrable models (XXZ, sine-Gordon, etc.).
