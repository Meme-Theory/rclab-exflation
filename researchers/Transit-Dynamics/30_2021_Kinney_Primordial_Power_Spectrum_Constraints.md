# Constraints on Inflationary Models from Primordial Power Spectrum

**Author(s):** William H. Kinney
**Year:** 2009-2021 (reviews)
**Journal:** Classical and Quantum Gravity, Physical Review D

---

## Abstract

William Kinney provides comprehensive reviews of constraints from primordial power spectrum measurements on inflationary models. Using Planck, WMAP, and large-scale structure data, he derives bounds on slow-roll parameters (ε, η), running of spectral index (α), and features in the spectrum. Modern data constrain inflation to an increasingly narrow window, challenging many theoretical models.

---

## Key Constraints (Planck 2018)

- Spectral index: n_s = 0.9661 ± 0.0040 (scale-invariant favored)
- Running: α = dn_s/d ln k = 0.003 ± 0.007 (consistent with zero)
- Tensor ratio: r < 0.058 (upper limit; no tensor detection)
- Local bispectrum: f_NL = −26 ± 55 (consistent with zero)
- Equilateral bispectrum: f_NL^{eq} = −42 ± 75

---

## Implications for Inflation

1. **Scale-Invariance Robust**: Data strongly prefer n_s ≈ 1, supporting theories predicting scale-invariance.

2. **No Running Detected**: α consistent with zero; rules out many slow-roll models predicting α ~ 10^{−3}.

3. **No Tensor Modes**: r < 0.06 rules out large-field inflation; favors small-field or non-inflationary models.

4. **Gaussianity**: f_NL ≈ 0 rules out non-Gaussian features; consistent with slow-roll or Bogoliubov pair creation (both predict f_NL ≈ 0).

---

## Key Results

1. **Inflation Still Viable**: Data consistent with inflation but constraint window tightening.

2. **Small-Field Inflation Preferred**: Large-field models (m²φ²/2, λφ⁴) increasingly disfavored due to r > 0.01 predictions.

3. **Starobinsky Inflation Success**: R² inflation's prediction n_s ≈ 0.966 matches Planck exceptionally well.

4. **Effective Field Theory**: Kinney shows inflation can be parameterized as low-energy EFT without specifying microscopic inflation model.
