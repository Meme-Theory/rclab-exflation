# Geometric Reheating After Inflation

**Author(s):** Bruce Bassett, Stefan Tsujikawa, David Wands
**Year:** 1997-2010 (key papers)
**Journal:** Physical Review D, Journal of Cosmology and Astroparticle Physics

---

## Abstract

Bassett showed that reheating can occur via non-minimal coupling to gravity (ξR term in action), producing copious particle creation without requiring explicit couplings to other fields. This "geometric reheating" is model-independent and occurs for generic inflatons.

---

## Key Mechanism

For a scalar field with Lagrangian:

L = (1/2)(∂φ)² − V(φ) − (ξ/2)R φ²

where ξ ~ 1/6 (conformal coupling) or larger, the effective mass becomes negative during inflation's exit, triggering tachyonic instability and explosive particle production.

---

## Key Results

1. **Efficient Reheating**: Geometric coupling can transfer inflaton energy rapidly into radiation.

2. **Robustness**: Works for any potential, not requiring fine-tuned interactions.

3. **Early Reheating**: Can reheat universe quickly, important for GUT-scale physics and baryogenesis.
