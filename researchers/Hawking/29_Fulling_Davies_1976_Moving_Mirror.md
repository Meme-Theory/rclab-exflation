## Key Arguments and Derivations

[INCOMPLETE - pre-arXiv, no PDF available]

The moving mirror model is a (1+1)-dimensional system where a perfectly reflecting boundary (mirror) follows a prescribed trajectory $z(t)$ in flat spacetime. Despite the absence of curvature, a time-dependent boundary condition causes particle creation from the vacuum via the same Bogoliubov transformation mechanism as in curved spacetime.

### Known Key Results

1. **Particle creation from boundary motion:** A mirror accelerating in (1+1)D Minkowski space radiates particles. The radiation spectrum depends on the mirror trajectory $z(t)$.

2. **Conformal anomaly:** The stress-energy tensor acquires a trace anomaly proportional to the Ricci scalar when computed on the conformally-related spacetime. In the moving mirror context, this manifests as the Schwarzian derivative of the trajectory mapping.

3. **Thermal spectrum for exponential Doppler:** When the mirror trajectory approaches a null asymptote (constant proper acceleration), the radiation becomes thermal with temperature $T = \hbar a / (2\pi k_B c)$, reproducing the Unruh result.

4. **Energy flux:** The energy flux radiated by the mirror is proportional to the Schwarzian derivative:
$$\langle T_{uu} \rangle = -\frac{1}{24\pi}\{p(u), u\}$$
where $p(u)$ is the ray-tracing function relating outgoing to incoming null coordinates and $\{f, u\} = f'''/f' - \frac{3}{2}(f''/f')^2$ is the Schwarzian derivative.

5. **Analog for black hole formation:** A mirror that starts at rest and asymptotically approaches a null line mimics the formation of a black hole horizon. The late-time radiation is thermal at the Hawking temperature.

1. Moving mirror in (1+1)D flat spacetime creates particles via time-dependent boundary conditions
