### Known Key Results

The Hawking-Page paper established the existence of a first-order phase transition in the thermodynamics of black holes in anti-de Sitter (AdS) space. The key results, which define the "Hawking-Page transition," are:

1. **AdS black hole thermodynamics:** In AdS space with cosmological constant $\Lambda = -3/\ell^2$, the Schwarzschild-AdS black hole has a minimum temperature. Unlike asymptotically flat black holes (which have negative specific heat and are thermodynamically unstable), large AdS black holes have positive specific heat and can be in thermal equilibrium.

2. **Two branches of black holes:** For temperatures above the minimum temperature $T_{\min}$, there exist two black hole solutions: a large (stable) black hole with positive specific heat and a small (unstable) black hole with negative specific heat.

3. **Phase transition:** At a critical temperature $T_{\text{HP}}$, there is a first-order phase transition between thermal radiation in AdS (thermal AdS) and the large stable black hole. Below $T_{\text{HP}}$, thermal AdS dominates; above $T_{\text{HP}}$, the large black hole dominates.

4. **Critical temperature:** The Hawking-Page temperature is:
$$T_{\text{HP}} = \frac{1}{\pi\ell}$$
where $\ell$ is the AdS radius.

5. **Free energy crossing:** The transition occurs when the free energies of thermal AdS and the large black hole become equal. The free energy of the Schwarzschild-AdS black hole is:
$$F = M - TS = \frac{r_+}{4}\left(1 - \frac{r_+^2}{\ell^2}\right)$$
where $r_+$ is the horizon radius. $F = 0$ at $r_+ = \ell$ (the transition point).

6. **Euclidean path integral interpretation:** The partition function is computed via the Euclidean gravitational path integral. The dominant saddle-point changes from thermal AdS (no horizon) to the Euclidean black hole at $T_{\text{HP}}$.

## Key Results

1. First-order phase transition between thermal AdS and large Schwarzschild-AdS black hole at $T_{\text{HP}} = 1/(\pi\ell)$
2. Large AdS black holes have positive specific heat (thermodynamically stable)
3. Small AdS black holes have negative specific heat (thermodynamically unstable)
4. The transition is interpreted via the Euclidean path integral as a change of dominant saddle
5. This work became foundational for AdS/CFT: Witten (1998) reinterpreted the HP transition as a confinement-deconfinement transition in the dual gauge theory
