## Key Results

1. Discovered the Unruh effect: a uniformly accelerated observer in the Minkowski vacuum perceives a thermal bath of particles at the Unruh temperature $T_U = \hbar a / (2\pi c k_B)$, where $a$ is the proper acceleration.
2. Demonstrated the equivalence between the Minkowski vacuum (as seen by inertial observers) and a thermal state (as seen by Rindler observers), establishing that the notion of "particle" is observer-dependent.
3. The Unruh effect arises from the same Bogoliubov transformation mechanism as Hawking radiation: the Rindler and Minkowski mode decompositions are related by Bogoliubov coefficients that mix positive and negative frequencies.
4. Showed the deep connection: the Hawking temperature $T_H = \hbar\kappa/(2\pi)$ and the Unruh temperature $T_U = \hbar a/(2\pi)$ have the same form, with surface gravity $\kappa$ playing the role of acceleration $a$.
5. Introduced the concept that the Minkowski vacuum, when restricted to the Rindler wedge, is a thermal density matrix obtained by tracing over the degrees of freedom behind the Rindler horizon.
6. This tracing mechanism became the template for understanding Hawking radiation as entanglement between interior and exterior modes of the black hole.
