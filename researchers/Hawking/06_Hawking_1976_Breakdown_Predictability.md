## Key Results

1. Argued that the formation and evaporation of black holes causes a fundamental breakdown of quantum predictability: pure states evolve into mixed states, violating unitarity.
2. Introduced the superscattering operator $\$$ (dollar matrix) that maps initial density matrices to final density matrices: $\rho_{\text{final}} = \$ \cdot \rho_{\text{initial}}$. The $\$$-matrix generalizes the $S$-matrix when unitarity is lost.
3. The $\$$-matrix factorizes as $\$_{AB,CD} = S_{AC} S^*_{BD}$ only when unitarity holds. When information is lost, the factorization fails.
4. Argued that the outgoing radiation is in a mixed state because it is entangled with the interior of the black hole, which disappears upon complete evaporation. Tracing over the interior degrees of freedom produces the thermal density matrix.
5. Identified the information paradox explicitly: either (a) information is truly lost and quantum mechanics is modified, (b) information comes out in the radiation via subtle correlations, or (c) a remnant stores the information.
6. Hawking originally advocated option (a) in this paper. He reversed his position in 2005 (Paper 10).
