## Abstract

Cosmological gravitational particle production (CGPP) is the creation of particles in an expanding universe due solely to their gravitational interaction. These particles can play an important role in the cosmic history through their connection to various cosmological relics including dark matter, gravitational-wave radiation, dark radiation, and the baryon asymmetry. This review explains the phenomenon of CGPP as a consequence of quantum fields in a time-dependent background, catalogs known results for the spectra and cosmological abundance of gravitationally produced particles of various spins, and explores the phenomenological consequences and observational signatures of CGPP.

---

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### Theoretical Framework (Sec. III)

**Quantum scalar field in FLRW spacetime:** A free scalar field $\phi$ in an FLRW background with scale factor $a(t)$ satisfies the Klein-Gordon equation with time-dependent effective mass. The field is expanded in mode functions $v_k(\eta)$ satisfying:
$$v_k'' + \omega_k^2(\eta) v_k = 0, \quad \omega_k^2 = k^2 + m^2 a^2 - \left(\xi - \frac{1}{6}\right) a^2 R$$
where $\eta$ is conformal time, $\xi$ is the non-minimal coupling, and $R$ is the Ricci scalar.

**Bogoliubov transformations:** Particle creation occurs because the notion of "vacuum" changes as the background evolves. Mode functions at different times are related by Bogoliubov coefficients $\alpha_k, \beta_k$ with $|\alpha_k|^2 - |\beta_k|^2 = 1$. The particle number spectrum is $n_k = |\beta_k|^2$.

**Conformal symmetry breaking requirement:** CGPP requires broken conformal symmetry. Conformally-coupled massless fields ($\xi = 1/6$, $m = 0$) are not produced. Sources of conformal breaking include: mass, non-minimal coupling, spin $> 1/2$ with mass (longitudinal modes), and non-conformal coupling.

### CGPP for Various Spins (Secs. IV-V)

The review catalogs CGPP results for all spins:

- **Spin-0:** Standard Bogoliubov calculation. Minimal coupling ($\xi = 0$) gives maximal production. Spectrum peaks at $k/a \sim H_{\text{end}}$.
- **Spin-1/2:** Parallel procedure with Dirac equation in curved space. Mass provides the conformal-breaking parameter.
- **Spin-1:** Proca field. Longitudinal mode provides additional conformal breaking beyond mass alone, enhancing production.
- **Spin-3/2:** Rarita-Schwinger field. Enhanced production from longitudinal gravitino mode.
- **Spin-2:** Massive spin-2 field. Five polarization modes with different production efficiencies.

### Cosmological Abundance (Sec. IV.C)

The present-day abundance from CGPP scales as:
$$\Omega_\chi h^2 \propto \frac{m_\chi}{H_{\text{end}}} \cdot \left(\frac{H_{\text{end}}}{M_{\text{Pl}}}\right)^2 \cdot f(m_\chi/H_{\text{end}}, \xi, s)$$
where $H_{\text{end}}$ is the Hubble rate at the end of inflation, $m_\chi$ is the particle mass, $\xi$ is the non-minimal coupling, and $s$ is the spin. CGPP can produce particles with mass up to $\sim 10^{14}$ GeV even when the reheat temperature is much lower.

### Phenomenological Consequences (Sec. VI)

- **Dark matter:** CGPP provides a production mechanism for superheavy dark matter (WIMPzillas) that interact only gravitationally
- **Gravitational reheating:** CGPP can reheat the universe even without inflaton couplings to the Standard Model
- **Baryogenesis:** Gravitationally produced heavy particles can decay asymmetrically to generate baryon asymmetry
- **Dark radiation:** Light species produced by CGPP contribute to $\Delta N_{\text{eff}}$
- **Gravitational waves:** Tensor perturbations from CGPP contribute to the stochastic gravitational wave background
- **Primordial black holes:** Density perturbations from CGPP can seed PBH formation

## Key Results

1. CGPP is universal: any particle species with broken conformal invariance is produced by the expanding universe, regardless of its non-gravitational interactions
2. Production efficiency depends on spin: higher-spin particles with longitudinal modes experience enhanced production
3. CGPP can produce particles with mass up to $\sim H_{\text{end}} \sim 10^{14}$ GeV, far beyond thermal production reach
4. The mechanism provides a natural origin for superheavy dark matter (WIMPzillas)
5. CGPP can reheat the universe purely through gravitational particle production
6. Observational signatures include isocurvature perturbations, non-Gaussianity, and small-scale structure modifications
