## Abstract

In the early sixties Leonard Parker discovered that the expansion of the universe can create particles out of the vacuum, opening a new and fruitful field in physics. We give a historical review in the form of an interview that took place during the Conference ERE2014 (Valencia 1-5, September, 2014).

## Key Arguments and Derivations

### Section I: Preamble

Parker is identified as the pioneer of quantized fields in curved spacetime. His breakthrough discovery that the expansion of the universe can spontaneously produce particles from the vacuum was developed in his Ph.D. thesis (Harvard, 1966) and published in PRL (1968) and Physical Review (1969).

### Section II: The Discovery of Cosmological Particle Creation

**Method:** Parker evolved free quantum fields from an initial Minkowski spacetime with constant $a(t) = a_1$ through a general expansion $a(t)$ to a final constant $a(t) = a_2$. Using the theorem for general solutions of second-order ODEs, creation and annihilation operators in the initial vacuum each evolved into superpositions of creation and annihilation operators -- a Bogoliubov transformation. This proved unambiguously that particles were created from the initial vacuum.

**Conformal invariance and no-particle-creation theorem:** Parker showed that for all spins, no massless particles satisfying Penrose's conformally-invariant field equations are created in any smoothly and isotropically expanding universe (which are conformally flat). This was published in 1968 and 1969.

**Graviton creation:** Einstein gravitons (spin-2) satisfy the minimally-coupled scalar field equation in the Lifshitz gauge (Lifshitz, 1946), not the conformally-invariant spin-2 equation. Therefore gravitons ARE created in isotropically expanding universes. Ford and Parker (1977) explicitly quantized and demonstrated this.

**Deep consistency:** Parker's 1969 paper pointed out that massless scalar particle creation goes to zero in the radiation-dominated universe and massive particle creation goes to zero in the dust-filled universe -- revealing a "deep consistency" between general relativity and quantum field theory.

**Connection to inflation:** Parker calculated that the density of created particles depends on terms like $(\dot{a}/a)^2$ and $\ddot{a}/a$, which are large in the early universe. In 1975 he published a paper in Nature showing that a class of early expanding universes produces a thermal spectrum of particles. In 2008 with Glenz, he showed that the initial Minkowski vacuum evolves into the Bunch-Davies vacuum of inflationary models, using 200-digit precision Bogoliubov coefficient calculations to accommodate 60+ e-foldings.

### Trans-Planckian Universality

Parker emphasizes that the same Bogoliubov transformation mechanism underlies: (1) particle creation by expanding universes, (2) Hawking radiation from black holes, (3) the Unruh effect for accelerated observers, and (4) the Fulling-Rindler vacuum. All are manifestations of creation/annihilation operator mixing by time-dependent or observer-dependent backgrounds.

1. Expansion of the universe creates particles from the vacuum via Bogoliubov transformation of creation/annihilation operators (Parker, 1966-1969)
2. Conformally-invariant massless fields of any spin produce zero particles in conformally-flat (isotropic) expansion
3. Einstein gravitons ARE created by isotropic expansion because linearized gravity is NOT conformally invariant
4. Particle creation shows a "deep consistency" between GR and QFT: the created spectrum is self-consistent with the spacetime that produces it
5. The initial Minkowski vacuum evolves into the Bunch-Davies vacuum of inflation to 200+ digit accuracy
6. Particle creation back-reaction can cause cosmological "bounces," avoiding singularities
