## Key Results

1. Established the four laws of black hole mechanics in precise analogy with the four laws of thermodynamics:
   - **Zeroth law**: The surface gravity $\kappa$ is constant over the event horizon of a stationary black hole.
   - **First law**: For nearby stationary black hole solutions, $\delta M = \frac{\kappa}{8\pi} \delta A + \Omega_H \delta J + \Phi_H \delta Q$.
   - **Second law**: The area of the event horizon never decreases, $\delta A \geq 0$ (Hawking's area theorem).
   - **Third law**: It is impossible to reduce the surface gravity $\kappa$ to zero by any finite sequence of operations.
2. At the time, the authors regarded the thermodynamic analogy as purely formal. Hawking's 1974-75 discovery of black hole radiation promoted it to a physical identity with $T = \hbar\kappa/(2\pi)$ and $S = A/(4\ell_P^2)$.
3. The first law is a Smarr-type differential identity holding for perturbations between neighboring Kerr-Newman solutions.
