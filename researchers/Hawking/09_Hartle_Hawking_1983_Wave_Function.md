## Key Results

1. Proposed that the quantum state of the universe is defined by a Euclidean path integral over compact 4-geometries with a single boundary: the no-boundary proposal.
2. The wave function of the universe $\Psi[h_{ij}, \phi]$ is a functional of the 3-metric $h_{ij}$ and matter field configuration $\phi$ on a closed 3-surface $\Sigma$:
   $$\Psi[h_{ij}, \phi] = \int \mathcal{D}g\,\mathcal{D}\Phi\; e^{-I_E[g,\Phi]}$$
   where the integral is over compact 4-geometries $(M, g)$ whose only boundary is $\Sigma$ with induced metric $h_{ij}$.
3. The no-boundary condition replaces the initial singularity: there is no boundary at the "beginning" -- spacetime is smooth and closed, like the South Pole of a sphere.
4. Applied the proposal to a minisuperspace model (homogeneous, isotropic universe with cosmological constant), obtaining a wave function that predicts an inflationary phase.
5. The wave function satisfies the Wheeler-DeWitt equation $\hat{H}\Psi = 0$.
6. The proposal gives definite (not arbitrary) initial conditions for the universe, determined entirely by the geometry of the path integral.
