## Key Results

1. Proved the area theorem (second law of black hole mechanics): the total area of event horizons can never decrease in any classical process, assuming the weak energy condition $T_{ab} k^a k^b \geq 0$ and cosmic censorship.
2. Applied the area theorem to constrain the maximum energy radiated in gravitational waves from black hole collisions: $\Delta E \leq M_1 + M_2 - M_{\text{final}}$ where $A_{\text{final}} \geq A_1 + A_2$.
3. For two Schwarzschild black holes of equal mass $M$, the maximum fraction of mass radiated is $1 - 1/\sqrt{2} \approx 29\%$.
4. Established the formal analogy between horizon area and thermodynamic entropy that Bekenstein later promoted to a physical identification.
