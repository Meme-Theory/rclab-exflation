## Key Results

1. Proposed that black holes carry entropy proportional to their horizon area: $S_{\text{BH}} = \eta \frac{k_B c^3}{\hbar G} A$, where $\eta$ is a dimensionless constant of order unity.
2. The generalized second law (GSL): the total entropy $S_{\text{total}} = S_{\text{ordinary}} + S_{\text{BH}}$ never decreases in any process. This extends the ordinary second law of thermodynamics to include black hole entropy.
3. Argued on information-theoretic grounds that a black hole of area $A$ has $\exp(S_{\text{BH}}/k_B)$ internal microstates, establishing the holographic nature of gravitational entropy (entropy scales as area, not volume).
4. The precise coefficient $\eta = 1/4$ was not determined in this paper; it was fixed by Hawking's 1975 calculation of the black hole temperature, giving $S_{\text{BH}} = A/(4\ell_P^2)$ where $\ell_P = \sqrt{\hbar G/c^3}$.
5. Used Gedanken experiments (lowering boxes of radiation into black holes) to argue that any violation of the GSL would allow a perpetual motion machine of the second kind.
6. Established the Bekenstein bound: the entropy of a system of energy $E$ confined to a sphere of radius $R$ satisfies $S \leq 2\pi k_B R E / (\hbar c)$.
