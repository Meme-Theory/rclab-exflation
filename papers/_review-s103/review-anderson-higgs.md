## §2 What Survives

Essentially all of it. This paper is in a structurally different class from the four cosmology-leaning papers: its content is a **theorem about the NCG inner-fluctuation construction**, not a cosmological prediction subject to observational drift. The survivors:

1. **The main theorem (thm:main) is PERMANENT.** It is W12 in the wall catalog, closed-mechanism #50, and atlas-07 A6 (Level A: "Genuinely Novel — publishable standalone"). It has survived S51→S103 with no retraction or qualification anywhere in atlas-09 (the retraction log has 49 entries; not one touches U(1)_7 ungaugeability, the commutant property, or the categorical argument).

2. **The commutant identity `[iK_7,D_K(τ)]=0` is exact and machine-verified.** `ratio_bare = 0.0` EXACTLY in the npz (not just below threshold). The all-orders extension (eq numverify: 1.3e-17 / 3.0e-17 / 3.1e-16) is the genuine computational ground truth and matches the paper bit-for-bit.

3. **The `D_phys` breaking value (0.117) is correct.** I re-ran the npz this review: `epsilon_K7 = 0.11685156604730382`. The paper quotes 0.117. The framework's own S51 script carries a *stale* "0.052" comment that the paper correctly did NOT propagate — the paper is more accurate than the script's prose. Remark 4.5's logic (the breaking comes from A_F fluctuations, not from K_7 becoming gaugeable; Proof II is breaking-independent) is exactly the S51 Level-3b structural argument and is sound.

4. **The categorical argument (Proof II) is the load-bearing one and is independent of all framework internals.** It uses only: (i) Gelfand-Naimark `Aut(C^∞(M)) ≅ Diff(M)`, (ii) `Inn(commutative) = {id}`, (iii) gauge fields ⇐ `Inn(A_F)`. This is pure Connes-NCG, holds for *any* compact Lie group K and *any* Killing field, and needs no Jensen deformation, no τ, no eigenvalue cache. For the NCG-community audience this is the paper's spine, and it stands fully self-contained.

5. **The KK-spectral-triple generalization (§5.2) is a genuine structural contribution.** "Any isometry of K that commutes with D_K is automatically ungaugeable; the commutant condition is simultaneously the signature of an isometry (Kosmann-Lichnerowicz) and the obstruction to gauging (trivial one-form)." This dual identity is the real novel content and is correct.

6. **The order-counting observation (§6.2): Kosmann derivatives are first-order operators, inner fluctuations `a[D,b]` are zeroth-order.** This is a clean, correct, independent perspective that complements Proof II. It survives.

## §3 What Must Change

Ordered by severity. Items here are paper-side fixes (the physics is intact; these are accuracy, framing, and self-containment fixes for the target venue).

### 3.1 The "two arguments" vs register's "three proofs" — resolve in the paper's favor, but disclose the lineage

The register (atlas-07 A6, baseline-findings, constraint-mega-matrix) describes the result as having **three independent proofs: commutant, categorical, numerical**. The paper presents **two structurally distinct arguments** (commutant — with its all-orders extension — and categorical). 

This is NOT a defect in the paper; it is a *correction* of the register. The S51 script's "three levels" (Level 1 tree / Level 2 one-loop / Level 3 off-diagonal) are all sub-parts of the *commutant* argument, and "numerical" was always verification, never an independent proof. The paper's reorganization (commutant-with-non-perturbative-extension + categorical) is cleaner and more honest.

**Action**: keep the two-argument structure. Add one sentence in §6.4 ("What is new") or a footnote noting that the all-orders extension (Cor 4.3) subsumes what earlier internal accounts split into separate "tree / one-loop / numerical" levels. Do NOT inflate to three proofs to match the register; instead the register's A6 description should be down-corrected (a housekeeping item, not a paper item). This is a Class-(c)-style stale-source situation in reverse: the paper is the current canonical, the register row is the stale description.

### 3.2 Internal-artifact dependencies must be made self-contained (NCG-community requirement)

The paper currently leans on internal computation citations (`needed:Session34computation`, `Session35computation`, `Session51computation`, `Session12computation`, `Session10computation`, `Session50computation`) for load-bearing facts. For JNCG, a reader has no access to `s34a_dphys_fold.py`. Each internal-cited fact must either (a) be re-derived in-text from first principles, or (b) be presented as an explicitly-labeled "numerical illustration" subordinate to a self-contained mathematical statement.

Specifically:
- **Prop 3.1** (the commutant identity) has a complete analytic proof in-text (the `ad(λ_7)`-invariance argument) — GOOD. The numerical line is illustration; label it as such. The analytic proof is what carries the theorem; the 79,968-pair census is corroboration.
- **Cor 4.3** (non-perturbative extension) has a complete proof (double-commutant theorem) — GOOD. The eq (numverify) ratios are illustration.
- **Remark 4.5** (the 0.117 breaking) is a numerical observation with no analytic counterpart. This is fine *as a remark* but must be framed as "we observe numerically that…", not as a load-bearing step. (It is not load-bearing: the theorem is breaking-independent by Proof II.)
- **§5.1** (ω_G ≈ 0.070, BCS condensate K_7=±1/2) is the most artifact-dependent section. For JNCG this is the *physics-application* section and should be clearly demarcated as "consequences in the phonon-exflation model" so a pure-NCG reader can take the theorem without buying the application. Consider moving §5.1 detail to a clearly-flagged "Application" subsection.

### 3.3 Drift fix: the residual isometry is `U(2)_R ⊃ U(1)_7`, not bare `U(1)_7` (claims #12, #13)

eq (isometrybreaking) writes `SU(3)×SU(3) → SU(3)_L × U(1)_7`. The S96 W5-6 result (capstone §1.1) is that the Jensen-metric Killing stabilizer is `SU(3)_L × U(2)_R` (dim 12), with the SU(2) in the *right* factor. U(1)_7 is the abelian piece of U(2)_R that the BCS condensate breaks.

This does not damage the theorem (U(1)_7 ⊂ U(2)_R is a genuine residual isometry, λ_7 *is* Killing for g_τ, the commutant identity holds). But the *stated* symmetry-breaking pattern understates the residual right-isometry. **Action**: correct eq (isometrybreaking) to show the full `SU(3)_L × U(2)_R` stabilizer, then specialize to the U(1)_7 ⊂ U(2)_R subgroup that the rest of the paper studies. This also makes the paper consistent with Baptista 2306.01049 (which the paper cites), where the SU(3) unravelling breaks `(SU(3)×SU(3))/Z_3 → (SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1))/Z_6` — i.e. the SU(2)×U(1) on the right is exactly Baptista's residual, and the paper should align its eq with that.

### 3.4 Phononic-framing / container-thinking violations

Per `.claude/rules/phononic-framing.md`, flag where the paper treats the internal space as a container rather than the substrate. For a JNCG paper this is *largely acceptable* — the NCG-community register is explicitly geometric, and "fields on the internal space K" is standard NCG language, not a framework error. However, two spots cross into substantive container-thinking that a framework-aware reader (or a referee who has seen the capstone) would flag:

- **§5.2 "In a Kaluza–Klein geometry M⁴ × K where K is a compact internal manifold, the isometry group Iso(K) acts on the spectrum of the internal Dirac operator."** This is container framing ("fields on K"). The substrate reading: K IS the spectral content of D_K; there is no "internal manifold" the fields live *on*. **Recommendation**: for the JNCG audience, this is *tolerable* as standard KK language — but add a one-clause substrate footnote so the paper is consistent with the framework's own §"IS Space, Not IN Space" mandate. This is GEOMETRIC-class content (it concerns the spectral triple structure / D_K eigenvalues), so the substrate reframe is light-touch, not a rewrite.
- **§2.4 / Def 2.4**: "The Hilbert space is `L²(SU(3),Σ) ⊗ H_F`" — standard and fine. No container violation.

**Classification of the paper as a whole**: GEOMETRIC / PARTICLE. The paper concerns the spectral-triple structure (the automorphism classification of D_K, the commutant, the one-form module Ω¹_D) and the representation-theoretic content (the K_7 charge decomposition, selection rules). It is NOT a phononic-excitation result (no relay patterns, no GGE, no spectral-moment cosmology) and NOT a cosmological-prediction result. The container-thinking risk is therefore LOW and the §3.4 fixes are footnote-level, not structural. Mark the paper GEOMETRIC in any framework cross-index.

### 3.5 Leggett/Goldstone label reconciliation (claim #19)

§5.1 calls ω_G ≈ 0.070 M_KK "the Goldstone mode." The canonical object at that frequency is the **Leggett mode ω_L1** (LEGGETT-MODE-48; atlas-07 §VII "[NEW S48] Leggett mode omega_L1 = 0.070 M_KK"). These are the same number, and in the framework the U(1)_7-breaking Goldstone *is* realized as the Leggett-channel mode. **Action**: one sentence reconciling "the U(1)_7 Goldstone mode is the Leggett mode ω_L1 = 0.070 M_KK (S48–S50)" so the identification is explicit and the citation is to the right internal object.

### 3.6 Self-contained statement of "what is gauged" for the abstract

The abstract is strong but the phrase "the Standard Model algebra" should be expanded once to make the result legible to a referee who is NOT steeped in the framework: the point is that `Inn(A_F)` (the SM gauge group) gauges fine, while `Aut(C^∞(SU(3)))` (diffeomorphisms, including the Kosmann symmetry) does not — and U(1)_7 is on the wrong side of that line. This is already in §2.6 ("the SM gauge symmetries CAN be gauged … but U(1)_7 CANNOT"); lift one clause of it into the abstract.

## §4 New Results Since the Paper's Era That Belong In It

The paper was written ~S51. The S52–S103 record adds several items that strengthen or contextualize the result and should be folded in.

### 4.1 The Killing / non-Killing Higgs dichotomy (capstone §1.1) — the most important addition

The framework's own statement of the inner-fluctuation decomposition is now sharp (capstone §1.1): *"the one-form decomposes automatically into a spin-1 part (gauge fields, along the Killing directions) and a spin-0 part (the Higgs, along the non-Killing directions)."*

This is the deep context for the paper's corollary. U(1)_7 is generated by a **Killing** field (λ_7 is Killing for g_τ — that is exactly why [K_7,D_K]=0). Killing directions give spin-1 gauge fields *only*; but K_7's gauge field is the trivial one-form (commutant), so U(1)_7 gets neither a gauge boson (commutant kills the spin-1 part) nor a Higgs (it is Killing, not non-Killing). **This is why the Anderson-Higgs mechanism has nothing to work with.** The paper should state this explicitly: the ungaugeability is the Killing-side shadow of the framework's non-Killing Higgs mechanism.

### 4.2 S102 Higgs route-pin — cross-reference for Cor 4.4

S102 W4-20 pinned `m_H = 131.8 GeV` from KK-threshold corrections to the |S|² mode (`m_H_FW_KK_threshold`; Route-B forced; Aitken 127.5 ruled spurious). The framework Higgs is a **non-Killing** inner fluctuation (the |S|² transverse-oscillation mode of the fiber embedding). The paper's Cor 4.4 ("no Higgs mechanism for U(1)_7") is fully consistent: the real Higgs lives in the non-Killing sector, U(1)_7 in the Killing sector. **Action**: a one-paragraph cross-reference in §5 noting that the framework DOES have a Higgs (m_H=131.8 GeV, S102), it just does not couple to U(1)_7 — the impossibility is specific to the Killing-commutant U(1)_7, not a general failure of mass generation.

### 4.3 Baptista 2506.09126 (2025) — the complementary non-Killing result

The paper cites Baptista's KK work (`needed:Baptista2024KK`, `needed:Baptista2025chiral`). The 2025 paper (arXiv:2506.09126, "Chiral interactions of fermions and massive gauge fields in KK models") proves the *complementary* statement: gauge fields linked to **non-Killing** fields on K have massive, arbitrarily-light bosons and chiral couplings. Together with this paper they bracket the dichotomy: **Killing → ungaugeable (this paper); non-Killing → massive gauge boson, no ad-hoc Higgs (Baptista).** This is a strong framing point for §6.2 and should be made explicit — the two papers are the two halves of one structural statement about Iso(K) vs Diff(K) in KK spectral triples.

### 4.4 Strengthened commutant context

The registry (line 21515) now uses the K_7 commutant identity as the regime-free basis for a K_7-invariant-rank bound (`m_min_sector00 = 2`), and the result feeds the §VII.AD Δ_0 localization and §VII.AJ partition-stability theorems. The paper need not cite these (they are downstream framework results, not NCG-community content), but it confirms the commutant identity is load-bearing across many subsequent results — evidence the theorem is robust and central, worth a sentence in the conclusion's "further directions" (the paper already flags "classification of all commutant elements" — note that S88+ partition-stability work is exactly this program in the framework).
