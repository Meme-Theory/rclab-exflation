The document's collapse `τ → g_τ → D_K(τ) → S[D_K(τ),f,Λ] → observables` (§1.3) is, read geometrically, a statement that **the entire physics lives on a one-parameter family of self-adjoint operators**. The framework's restriction to the *single* unstable transverse-traceless Jensen direction (§2.1) means the parameter space is, at the working level, **1-dimensional** — and a 1D base carries **no Berry curvature 2-form by dimension count alone** (you need at least a 2-parameter base for `F = dA` to have a place to live). ### II.2 Band-lifting and the moving curvature — the §2.4 / §4.2 connection is exactly right

**This is the same statement read at two levels, and both are mine.** At the eigenbundle level: at a maximally symmetric point the bands touch (a degeneracy); the instant the symmetry-breaking parameter turns on, the degeneracy lifts and the eigenspaces become distinct sub-bundles. At the moment level: `{1, R_K, R_K²}` are independent functions iff `R_K` is *moving* (`R_K′ ≠ 0`); the Wronskian of powers of a moving scalar is `∝ R_K′³` for the elementary reason the document gives. The framework's identification of these two as the *same* band-lifting is correct and is a genuinely elegant piece of structural reasoning. The geometric-phase reading: an avoided crossing is the canonical site where the adiabatic connection becomes *singular in the adiabatic limit* — the Berry curvature of a two-level avoided crossing is sharply peaked at the gap minimum (BP-4: `B_n = −Im Σ_{m≠n} ⟨n|dH|m⟩⟨m|dH|n⟩/(E_n−E_m)²`, peaked where `E_n−E_m` is smallest).
