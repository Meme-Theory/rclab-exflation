## Scope

This file is the registry-class calibration corpus for the **discriminating-content sub-classification** within the algebra-INVARIANT class of `cross-pillar-bridge-anatomy.md §"Algebra-axis orthogonality K-counter"` MANDATORY-at-K=3 (S87 W-2 R3 close).

The algebra-axis K-counter establishes that algebra-INVARIANT (spectrum-only functional) and algebra-DEPENDENT (state-pair functional) families are STRUCTURALLY ORTHOGONAL in identity-class membership. This corpus tracks a finer sub-distinction WITHIN the algebra-INVARIANT class: an INVARIANT result may be **vacuous** (algebraically forced by convention with no substrate content) OR **carry discriminating content** (at least one factor encodes substrate-IS structure whose counterfactual variants are structurally excluded by upstream PROVEN identities).

The distinction matters because INVARIANT-vacuous and INVARIANT-with-DISCRIMINATING-CONTENT have OPPOSITE epistemic weight under the falsifier-master-inventory schema-v2 directional-PASS reading: vacuous-INVARIANT sign-PASS is tautology-equivalent (no falsification value); DISCRIMINATING-CONTENT-INVARIANT sign-PASS is substrate-IS evidence (genuine falsification value, with counterfactual substrate variants explicitly identified as structurally excluded).

## Decision procedure (substitution-chain factor analysis)

Per W-5 §II.5 lines 130-138 (sagan synthesis):

1. **Decompose the result into N factors** via the substitution chain (per `math-scripts.md §"Double-Check Logic Before Compute"`). Each factor is a named quantity with a substrate-physics or convention-based source.
2. **For each factor, determine source class**: substrate-IS (factor value derives from `(A_K, H_K, D_K)` spectral identity, PROVEN structural theorem, or canonical_constants pin with substrate-first provenance) OR convention (factor value forced by algebraic identity, regulator-class choice, or scheme-dependent normalization).
3. **For each substrate-IS factor, identify counterfactual substrate variants** that would produce sign-FAIL or magnitude-FAIL on the parent observable. Document each counterfactual + the upstream PROVEN identity that structurally excludes it.
4. **Classification rule**:
   - If ≥ 1 substrate-IS factor with at least one identified-and-excluded counterfactual → **INVARIANT-with-DISCRIMINATING-CONTENT**
   - If all factors are convention-based with no substrate-IS content → **INVARIANT-vacuous**
   - If ≥ 1 factor is regulator-class-dependent / scheme-dependent → **DEPENDENT** (route to algebra-DEPENDENT class; out of corpus scope)

The decision procedure is structurally unambiguous when applied at plan-freeze with full substitution-chain factor enumeration. A claim of "INVARIANT" without per-factor substitution-chain analysis is **registry-incomplete** — the W-5 §V.5 corpus discipline requires the per-factor decomposition to land alongside the registry entry.

